7 Zapier Alternatives With Free (or Unlimited) Plans for Solo Creators (2026)

Last updated September 2026 · ~9 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools, and I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: if you can run a $5–7/mo server, self-hosted n8n is the end of the task-limit problem -- unlimited workflows, unlimited executions, $0 for the software. If you refuse to touch a server, Make's free plan (1,000 credits/month, multi-step allowed) stretches roughly 10x further than Zapier's free plan, which in September 2026 still caps you at 100 tasks/month and two-step Zaps only. And if you want hosted-but-flat pricing with no per-task anxiety, Pabbly Connect and Activepieces are the two real contenders.

Why Zapier's free plan runs out fast

I checked zapier.com/pricing again this week. The free plan gives you 100 tasks/month, two-step Zaps only (one trigger, one action), 15-minute polling, and Copilot with a daily message cap. Three things make those 100 tasks evaporate faster than you expect:

  • Every action step bills a task. One new lead that gets added to a sheet, tagged in your email tool, and posted to Slack is 3 tasks -- except on free you can't even build that, because it's a multi-step Zap, and multi-step requires Professional (from $19.99/mo on annual billing).
  • The useful stuff is gated, not metered. Webhooks, premium apps, and multi-step all live behind the paywall. The free plan isn't a small version of Zapier; it's a demo.
  • Zapier's 2026 pricing unified AI steps, code steps, and SDK calls into the same task meter. Add one AI formatting step to a Zap and your consumption roughly doubles per run.

To be fair: Zapier still has the deepest app catalog in the business (8,000+ integrations), and if your workflow touches some obscure CRM, it may be the only tool that connects without custom webhook work. You're not paying for nothing. You're paying per sneeze.

n8n: unlimited self-hosted automation (top pick)

n8n is the tool this site runs on, so you know my bias up front -- but the pricing logic is just arithmetic. The self-hosted Community Edition is free software with unlimited workflows and unlimited executions. Put it on a $5–7/mo VPS and a workflow that runs 50,000 times a month costs exactly the same as one that runs 50 times. On Zapier, 50,000 tasks/month is deep into three-figure territory.

Even n8n's paid Cloud tiers price differently from Zapier in a way that favors creators: you pay per full workflow execution, not per step. A 15-node monster workflow counts as 1 execution on n8n; the equivalent Zap bills ~15 tasks per run. Cloud Starter is $20/mo billed annually (about $24 month-to-month) for 2,500 executions; Pro is $50/mo annual for 10,000.

The honest cons: self-hosting means you are the sysadmin -- Docker, updates, backups, and the occasional 11pm "why did my webhook stop responding" session. The learning curve is real; n8n is node-based and technical compared to Zapier's forms. And its integration catalog (~1,200 nodes) is a fraction of Zapier's 8,000+, so long-tail SaaS apps sometimes need the HTTP Request node and an API doc open in another tab. My full setup walkthrough is in the how to self-host n8n cheap guide, the free n8n AI agent templates roundup shows what to import once it's running, and the n8n vs Zapier breakdown goes deeper on the pricing math.

Skip it if: the words "reverse proxy" make you close the tab. That's fine -- the next three tools are for you.

Make vs Zapier: free tier compared

Make's free plan is the best no-server free tier in automation, and it isn't close. 1,000 credits/month vs Zapier's 100 tasks, and -- the part that actually matters -- multi-step scenarios are allowed on free. The trigger-to-three-actions workflow that Zapier's free plan flatly refuses to build runs fine on Make at $0.

One 2026 change to know: Make moved from "operations" to "credits" as its metering unit, with each module action consuming one credit (code steps bill 2 credits per second of runtime). Functionally similar for simple scenarios, but read your usage screen for anything loop-heavy -- iterators can chew credits fast. The free plan also enforces a 15-minute minimum interval between scheduled runs and won't let you have more than a couple of active scenarios' worth of practical throughput.

Paid Make stays cheap: Core at $12/mo gets you 10,000 credits and unlimited active scenarios -- roughly what Zapier charges $19.99/mo to begin talking about, except Zapier's entry tier includes far fewer tasks (750 at the base Professional price point). For a visual-first, non-technical solo creator, Make is the default recommendation. The Make vs Zapier comparison covers the ease-of-use trade-offs in detail.

The honest cons: credit math on loops surprises people; the scenario editor is gorgeous but slower to build in than it looks; and error handling below the Pro tier is basic.

The other free-plan contenders

Activepieces: open source with a hosted free tier

Activepieces is the open-source middle path: self-host the Community Edition free and unmetered, or use the hosted free plan (1,000 credits/mo, unlimited flows). Heads up on a pricing change: the hosted flat plan is now Plus at $20/mo for 10,000 credits -- it was $16/mo earlier this year, so older comparisons (including one on this site) understate it. The quirk I dislike: free-plan credits reset daily rather than giving you a monthly pool, so a once-a-week heavy job can starve. Overage on paid is $0.007/credit, which is sane.

Pabbly Connect: the "unlimited" marketing is half true

Pabbly's pitch is lifetime deals and unlimited tasks, and here's the honest decode: the free plan is a tiny 100 tasks/mo, the Standard plan is 10,000 tasks at $14/mo (shown as marked down from $19/mo when I checked -- Pabbly runs discounts more or less permanently), and the genuinely interesting part is that triggers and internal steps (filters, routers, formatters) don't count as tasks, so the same workflow consumes 1 Pabbly task where Zapier bills 2-3. The Unlimited tier -- where runs don't consume a task meter at all -- is sold as a one-time-payment lifetime deal. Lifetime deals on infrastructure make me nervous as a rule (someone has to pay the servers in year four), but Pabbly has been running this model for years. Its weakness is integration depth: ~2,000 apps, and the long tail is thinner than the number suggests.

IFTTT: cheapest paid unlimited, simplest ceiling

IFTTT's free plan is 2 Applets with unlimited runs -- note the inversion: Zapier meters runs and not workflows, IFTTT meters workflows and not runs. Pro+ at $8.99/mo (billed annually) is unlimited Applets and unlimited runs, which makes it the cheapest hosted "never think about usage again" plan on this list. The catch is capability: Applets are simple trigger-action pairs with light filtering. It's the right tool for smart-home and personal automations, and the wrong tool the day you need a router, a loop, or an API call with a custom payload.

Node-RED: free forever, zero metering, zero hand-holding

Node-RED is the open-source flow tool that came out of IBM -- Apache-licensed, self-hosted, nothing is counted, ever. It runs happily on the same cheap VPS as n8n (or a Raspberry Pi on your desk). The trade: there's no SaaS app catalog in the Zapier sense; you wire HTTP, MQTT, and community nodes yourself. I'd point hardware tinkerers and IoT projects here, and point SaaS-workflow creators to n8n, which gives you the same $0 unlimited economics with far more prebuilt app nodes.

Pipedream: a warning, not a recommendation

Pipedream used to be the generous developer-friendly free tier everyone recommended. As of its 2026 restructure, the free plan is development-only -- running workflows in production for real users now requires the Startup plan at $99/mo (annual; $150 month-to-month). It's still excellent for prototyping against 3,000+ APIs, and the per-credit model is fine at business scale, but it no longer belongs on a solo creator's free-forever shortlist. I'm listing it so you don't waste an afternoon migrating to a free tier that can't go live.

Best paid flat-rate alternatives (no per-task pricing)

If the thing you hate about Zapier is specifically the meter -- the low-grade anxiety of every workflow run costing money -- these are the flat-rate escapes, cheapest first:

  • ~$5–7/mo: n8n or Node-RED on a VPS. Unlimited everything; you're paying for the server, not the software. See the n8n hosting options rundown for which plan actually fits.
  • $8.99/mo: IFTTT Pro+ (annual billing). Unlimited Applets and runs, hosted, zero maintenance -- if simple trigger-action automations are all you need.
  • $20/mo flat: Activepieces Plus. Hosted, open-source core, 10,000 credits with predictable overage -- the closest thing to "n8n economics without the sysadmin job."
  • One-time: Pabbly Connect Unlimited lifetime deals, with the caveat above about lifetime-deal risk.

Which one should you actually pick?

  • Comfortable with Docker, or willing to learn: self-hosted n8n. It's the only option here that scales from hobby to business without the bill scaling with it. Grab the free n8n templates to skip the blank-canvas phase.
  • Never touching a server, workflows under ~1,000 runs/mo: Make's free plan. Upgrade to Core at $12/mo when you outgrow it.
  • Want hosted + flat-rate + open-source escape hatch: Activepieces Plus at $20/mo.
  • Simple personal/smart-home automations only: IFTTT Pro+ at $8.99/mo, unlimited.
  • Allergic to subscriptions: Pabbly Connect's lifetime Unlimited -- eyes open about what lifetime deals are.

FAQ

What is the best free Zapier alternative in 2026?

Self-hosted n8n if you'll run a $5–7/mo VPS: unlimited workflows and executions, $0 software. If you won't touch a server, Make's free plan (1,000 credits/month with multi-step scenarios) stretches roughly 10x further than Zapier's 100-task, two-step-only free plan.

Does Zapier still limit free users to 100 tasks per month?

Yes -- as of September 2026 the free plan is 100 tasks/month, two-step Zaps only, with 15-minute polling. Multi-step Zaps, webhooks, and premium apps require Professional, from $19.99/mo on annual billing.

Which Zapier alternatives have truly unlimited plans?

Self-hosted open-source tools -- n8n Community Edition and Node-RED -- are unlimited at the software level; your only cost is the server. Among hosted options, Pabbly Connect's Unlimited plan doesn't meter workflow runs, and IFTTT Pro+ gives unlimited Applets and runs for simple automations. Zapier, Make, Activepieces, and Pipedream all meter usage.
