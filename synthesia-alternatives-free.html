Why people go looking for a Synthesia alternative

Synthesia repriced in 2026: plans now start at 18 dollars/mo (billed annually, 29 dollars/mo monthly) for the Starter tier, and that buys you exactly 10 minutes of finished video per month plus access to 125+ stock avatars -- no custom avatar included until you are on the annual plan. The free Basic tier gives 1,200 credits/mo (about 10 minutes of video), but strips downloads-with-logo, AI dubbing beyond the trial, and locks you to 9 stock avatars. For anyone producing more than a couple of short videos a month, that ceiling gets expensive fast, and that is the exact search intent behind "Synthesia alternatives free."

HeyGen deep-dive: the closest real swap

Checked directly against heygen.com/pricing, September 2026: Free is 0 dollars/mo, 3 videos/mo, up to 1 minute each, watermarked, access to 500+ stock avatars and Avatar IV. Creator is 29 dollars/mo (or 24 dollars/mo billed annually), 600 credits/mo, videos up to 30 minutes, 1080p export, watermark removed, voice cloning, 175+ languages, and unused credits roll over. That Creator tier at 29 dollars/mo for 30-minute videos and no watermark beats Synthesia Starter at 29 dollars/mo for 10 minutes flat -- if avatar realism and template variety matter less to you than raw video length per dollar, HeyGen is the straightforward swap. The trade-off: Synthesia avatars and multi-language dubbing are generally rated more polished for corporate training content specifically, so if that is your use case, do not switch on price alone -- test both free tiers first.

The free/self-hosted option that actually works

There is no self-hosted open-source equivalent to Synthesia or HeyGen that produces comparable AI avatar quality -- this is not an n8n-style situation where you can run your own server. The genuinely free path is recording yourself. A 0-dollar phone recording plus CapCut (free tier, no watermark) for cuts, captions, and b-roll will outperform a synthetic avatar for audience trust in almost every niche that is not multi-language corporate training. If your actual goal is "cheap video content for social," skip AI avatars entirely and use that route -- it is the option every one of these tools is quietly competing against.

The 3 things nobody mentions

  • Synthesia "18 dollars/mo" headline price only shows up if you pay annually. The real monthly-billed rate is 29 dollars/mo, same as HeyGen Creator plan -- but for 10 minutes of video instead of 30. Compare monthly-to-monthly, not Synthesia annual rate against a competitor monthly rate.
  • Both "free" plans use a credit system, not a flat minute count, and credits get consumed by more than just video length. Synthesia Basic plan 1,200 credits/mo also covers AI-generated video assets and dubbing minutes drawn from the same pool -- if you use those features, your usable video minutes drop below the advertised 10.
  • Pictory is not actually an avatar-video competitor -- it is a different category wearing the same search term. It turns scripts or blog posts into videos using stock footage and text overlays, not a talking AI avatar. If you need a face on screen, Pictory does not solve that; it solves faceless content, which is a different (and often better) answer than what most "Synthesia alternative" searches assume they want.

Who should skip switching entirely

If you are already on Synthesia for corporate training or multi-language localization and it is working, do not migrate on price alone -- Synthesia avatar library and dubbing quality are built for exactly that use case, and HeyGen cheaper-per-minute math does not matter if the output quality does not clear your bar. Test HeyGen free 3 videos first before committing budget either direction.

Verdict

Start with HeyGen free plan (3 videos/mo, no card required) to test if AI avatar video fits your content at all. If it does and you need real volume, HeyGen Creator (29 dollars/mo, 30 min of video) beats Synthesia Starter on a dollar-per-minute basis. If you do not strictly need an avatar, skip both and film yourself with a phone plus free editing tools -- it costs 0 dollars and usually performs better anyway.

FAQ

Is there a free alternative to Synthesia?

Yes -- HeyGen free plan gives you 3 AI avatar videos per month (up to 1 minute each, watermarked), which is actually more generous for testing than Synthesia free Basic plan, which caps at roughly 10 minutes of video per month but requires you to stay within its credit system. Neither free tier is enough for regular content production, just enough to test if AI avatar video fits your workflow.

Is Synthesia worth it in 2026?

Synthesia Starter plan is 29 dollars/mo (or 18 dollars/mo billed annually) for 10 minutes of video and only 125+ stock avatars -- worth it if you specifically need Synthesia avatar realism and multi-language dubbing for business training or localization content. If you just need occasional short-form video, HeyGen Creator plan (29 dollars/mo, 600 credits, up to 30-minute videos) or a free tool like Canva gets you further per dollar.

Can I make AI avatar videos for free?

Yes, on a limited basis -- both Synthesia (Basic, 0 dollars/mo, about 10 min/mo) and HeyGen (Free, 0 dollars/mo, 3 videos/mo up to 1 min each, watermarked) offer real free tiers with no credit card required. Neither is viable for regular publishing; they are built to let you test the tool before you hit a paywall. See my best AI video generator (no face needed) guide if avatars are not a requirement.
