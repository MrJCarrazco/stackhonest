Systeme.io vs ClickFunnels (2026): Which One Is Actually Free

Last updated September 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools -- I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: Systeme.io has a real, permanent free plan (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course, unlimited email, 0% transaction fees). ClickFunnels has no free plan at all -- its cheapest tier starts at $97/mo. If free is the actual requirement in your search, Systeme.io is the only one of the two that qualifies. ClickFunnels editor and funnel templates are more polished, and if budget genuinely is not the constraint, it is a capable tool -- but you are paying for that polish from the first day.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you want a genuinely free plan that does not expire, or if you want an all-in-one platform (funnels + email + courses + blog + community) at a fraction of ClickFunnels price once you do pay. Pick ClickFunnels if you have a marketing budget from day one, want the most polished drag-and-drop funnel editor in the category, or need its specific ecosystem of coaches, templates, and integrations built around it.

The gap is stark at the entry point: Systeme.io top-tier Unlimited plan at $97/mo costs the same as ClickFunnels cheapest paid plan. If you are comparing like-for-like feature depth, Systeme.io is routinely a third to a tenth of ClickFunnels price at every tier.

Ease of use: ClickFunnels still has the more polished editor

ClickFunnels built its reputation on drag-and-drop funnel building, and it shows -- the page editor is smoother, the template library is deeper, and its Smart Checkout and A/B testing tools are more mature. If you have watched ClickFunnels marketing content, the software mostly delivers on the promise of a fast, visual funnel builder.

Systeme.io editor is less refined by comparison -- it does the job, but you will notice rougher edges in the page builder than ClickFunnels years of UX investment. What Systeme.io gives up in polish it makes up in scope: funnels, email marketing, blog, online courses, community, booking calendar, and affiliate program management are all built into every plan including free, where ClickFunnels treats several of those (courses, community, affiliate center) as newer or higher-tier add-ons.

What each one is actually for

ClickFunnels whole design center is the sales funnel -- getting a visitor from ad click to purchase with the fewest possible friction points, with heavy emphasis on upsells, order bumps, and checkout optimization. It is built by and for direct-response marketers running paid traffic.

Systeme.io covers funnels too, but its scope is wider from the start -- it is positioned as a full business-in-a-box for course creators, coaches, and small online businesses who need funnels, email, and a course platform without stitching together three tools. If your business model is sell a course or coaching program and nurture an email list, Systeme.io free-to-affordable path covers that without the ClickFunnels price tag.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

ClickFunnels failure modes

  • No free plan means no real test drive. You commit $97/mo (or a short trial) before you know if the workflow fits your business.
  • Contact and email caps tighten fast at the entry tier. 10K contacts and 50K emails/mo on the $97 Launch plan sounds like a lot until a list-building funnel actually works.
  • Price scales aggressively. Jumping from Launch to Scale is a $100/mo step for contact and workspace headroom, not new core functionality.

Verdict by business type

  • Just starting out, tight or zero budget: Systeme.io free plan. Nothing to lose, and it covers funnels + email + one course.
  • Course creator or coach on a budget: Systeme.io Startup ($17/mo) -- the all-in-one scope is the point.
  • Running paid traffic at real volume, budget is not the constraint: ClickFunnels -- the funnel editor and checkout optimization tools are more mature.
  • Want the most polished builder and do not mind starting at $97/mo: ClickFunnels Launch.
  • Need automation beyond funnels (AI agents, complex workflows): Neither -- see my n8n vs Zapier or Make vs Zapier breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io really free?

Yes, and it never expires. The free plan gives you 2,000 contacts, 3 sales funnels, 1 course, 1 blog, unlimited emails, and 0% transaction fees, with no credit card required.

Does ClickFunnels have a free plan?

No. ClickFunnels cheapest plan (Launch) is $97/mo billed monthly, or $81/mo billed annually. There is a free trial period, but no permanent free tier.

Which is better for a small business just starting out?

Systeme.io, almost without exception, if budget matters. Its free plan is a genuinely usable all-in-one (funnels, email, courses, blog) with no time limit. ClickFunnels funnel builder and editor are more polished, but you are paying $97/mo minimum from day one to find that out.
