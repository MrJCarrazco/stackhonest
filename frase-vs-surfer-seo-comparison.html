Learning curve / ease of use

Frase leans toward the brief-and-outline workflow: type a target keyword, get a SERP-based outline and question list pulled from what is already ranking, then write inside its editor or export to Google Docs on the Professional tier. It is fast to get a usable first draft skeleton, though Starter running out of its 10-article monthly allowance is the most common complaint once you are publishing more than a couple of pieces a week. Surfer workflow centers more on scoring a draft against a live Content Score plus tracking whether that content actually shows up in AI answer engines -- real-time feedback as you type, and a smoother round-trip if your writers work in Google Docs. Neither takes more than an afternoon to learn; Frase gets you to a starting outline faster, Surfer gives tighter feedback once you are mid-draft plus the AI-visibility data most buyers are now paying for.

Capability deep-dive: briefs vs scoring

Frase core strength is speed to a usable content brief -- SERP analysis, competitor outline extraction, and AI-generated first drafts, all built around getting a writer started fast. In 2026 it has added real GEO tracking: visibility into whether your published content is actually getting cited by AI answer engines, which is a meaningfully different signal than a traditional SERP rank. Surfer core strength is depth of on-page scoring -- NLP-driven term suggestions, a Topical Map, and a Cannibalization Report for planning content clusters, all benchmarked as close to the category standard for "will this specific draft rank." Where Frase pulls ahead: lower cost per seat, faster brief generation, and built-in AI-search-visibility tracking without an add-on. Where Surfer pulls ahead: team collaboration tooling and the maturity of its scoring model once you are optimizing a near-final draft.

Where each one breaks in production

  • Frase: Starter is capped at one site, one person, and now explicitly 10 articles and 50 audit pages a month -- fine for a light solo pace, but you will hit the ceiling fast once you add a second writer, a second property, or publish more than a couple of posts a week, forcing a jump to the $103-129/mo Professional tier.
  • Surfer SEO: the price jump from Standard to Pro ($99 to $182/month) mostly doubles your AI-prompt-tracking refresh rate and frequency, not the document cap, which stays at 360 on both tiers -- you need Peace of Mind at $299/month before documents go unlimited.
