Systeme.io vs Wix (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated October 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools, and I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: these are different categories of tool wearing similar build-an-online-business marketing. Systeme.io is a funnel-and-course platform with email built in -- $0-97/mo flat, 0% transaction fees, the same price regardless of contact count within a plan limit. Wix is a general-purpose website builder -- genuinely the stronger choice for a fully custom-designed site, blog, or portfolio, with a huge app marketplace -- but e-commerce does not unlock until the Core plan ($29/mo), and there is no dedicated multi-step sales funnel builder or course-hosting-with-email-automation bundle at any tier. If you are building a marketing or portfolio website first and selling is secondary, Wix is the better tool. If selling a course or digital product directly is the whole point, Systeme.io free plan already does that from day one.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you need funnels, checkout, and course hosting bundled with email, and you want a flat price that does not climb as you add pages or storage. Pick Wix if the main goal is a fully custom-designed website -- blog, portfolio, local business site -- with e-commerce as a secondary feature, and you want access to Wix much larger design and app ecosystem.

The honest framing: Wix wins decisively on design freedom and general-purpose website building. Systeme.io wins decisively on wanting to sell one thing, right now, without stacking three subscriptions to do it. Comparing them head-to-head only makes sense if you are deciding which single tool to start a small online business with -- and if selling is the actual goal rather than a side feature of a marketing site, Systeme.io free plan gets you further for $0 than Wix does at $29/mo.

What Wix has that Systeme.io does not

Wix design editor and template library are genuinely a different league -- thousands of professionally designed templates, a mature drag-and-drop canvas, and an app marketplace with hundreds of third-party integrations (booking systems, advanced SEO tools, loyalty programs, HIPAA-compliant forms on Business plans). For anyone building a portfolio, local business site, blog, or marketing site where visual polish and customization matter more than a funnel-and-checkout flow, Wix is simply the better-built tool. Wix also supports up to 100 collaborators on Business Elite, useful for agencies managing client sites, something Systeme.io (aimed at solo creators) does not emphasize.

The catch: none of that polish includes a dedicated sales funnel system, and real e-commerce does not exist on Wix until you are already paying $29/mo (Core) -- $12/mo more than Systeme.io equivalent entry tier, which includes funnels and courses Wix still does not have at that price point.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

Wix failure modes

  • No e-commerce on the two cheapest plans. Free and Light ($17/mo) cannot accept payments at all -- you are paying $29/mo minimum (Core) before you can sell anything.
  • No dedicated funnel or upsell builder. E-commerce on Wix is a standard storefront; building a multi-step sales funnel with order bumps and one-click upsells means bolting on third-party apps, which stack cost and complexity.
  • Email marketing is a lightweight bolt-on, not a real ESP. The marketing suite tier (Light/Basic through Business/Advanced) is serviceable for newsletters but nowhere near a dedicated automation platform depth.
  • Switching away from Wix is painful. Wix sites are not portable the way a self-hosted site is -- there is real platform lock-in once a site is built out.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • Design flexibility is limited. Pages are templated and functional, not a creative canvas -- anyone wanting a highly customized visual brand site will find it restrictive compared to Wix.
  • No general content-site tooling. Systeme.io is not built for blogs-as-the-main-product, portfolios, or sites where selling is not the primary goal.
  • No app marketplace. Systeme.io is a closed, all-in-one system -- no equivalent to Wix hundreds of third-party app integrations.

Verdict by business type

  • Solo creator or small business selling a course, digital product, or service through a funnel: Systeme.io free plan -- the whole sell-and-deliver stack at $0, no e-commerce plan upgrade required.
  • Local business, portfolio, or blog where a polished custom-designed website is the priority: Wix Light or Core -- the design and app ecosystem are the real differentiators here.
  • Agency managing many client sites needing heavy collaboration and custom dev work: Wix Business Elite -- $159/mo buys developer tools and up to 100 collaborators Systeme.io does not attempt.
  • Need workflow automation beyond a website or funnel (custom API-driven agents): Neither -- see the n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than Wix?

For selling a course, digital product, or running a funnel, yes. Wix Light is $17/mo and does not include e-commerce at all -- you need Core at $29/mo just to accept payments, and real marketing automation does not show up until Business or Business Elite at $39-159/mo. Systeme.io free plan already includes a funnel builder, checkout with 0% transaction fees, 1 course, and unlimited email sends, with paid tiers at $17, $47, and $97/mo.

Does Wix include sales funnels or course hosting?

No. Wix is a general-purpose website builder with an app marketplace. It has e-commerce (starting on Core) and a site-based online courses app, but no purpose-built multi-step sales funnel builder with built-in checkout and upsell logic the way Systeme.io does at every tier, including free.

Which one is better for a solo creator selling a course or digital product?

Systeme.io, if selling directly is the main goal -- its free plan bundles funnel, checkout, and course hosting with zero transaction fees. Wix is the better pick if the priority is a fully custom-designed marketing website, blog, or portfolio first, with selling as a secondary feature; its design flexibility and app ecosystem are genuinely stronger than Systeme.io templated builder.
