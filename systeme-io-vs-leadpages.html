Systeme.io vs Leadpages (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated September 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools -- I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: these two tools are not really competing for the same job. Systeme.io is an all-in-one platform (funnels, email marketing, courses, a website builder) with a permanent free plan. Leadpages is a dedicated landing-page and conversion-rate-optimization (CRO) tool -- no email marketing, no funnels, no course platform, no free plan, only a 7-day trial. If you need one tool to run the whole business, Systeme.io wins on price and scope. If you already have email and funnels covered and want the single best landing-page A/B-testing tool, Leadpages earns its premium.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you want funnels, email marketing, and a course platform bundled for free (or $17-97/mo as you scale) with zero transaction fees. Pick Leadpages if you specifically need best-in-class A/B testing, click/scroll heatmaps, Smart Traffic AI routing, and lead enrichment on landing pages -- and you already have an email/funnel tool covering the rest of the stack.

The comparison is lopsided because the tools are not aimed at the same job. If you priced out Leadpages Grow ($79/mo annual) plus a separate email tool plus a separate funnel/course platform, you would likely spend more than Systeme.io $97/mo Unlimited tier that already includes all three. But Leadpages actual product -- landing-page conversion optimization -- is more advanced than anything Systeme.io ships: Smart Traffic AI routing, click+scroll heatmaps, and lead enrichment (IP-level visitor identification) do not exist in Systeme.io at any price.

What Leadpages has that Systeme.io does not

Leadpages is not a worse Systeme.io -- it is a specialist tool with real depth Systeme.io does not match. Smart Traffic (AI-driven variant routing, from Optimize at $159/mo annual) automatically sends visitors to whichever page variant is converting best, without you running the stats yourself. Click and scroll heatmaps show exactly where visitors drop off. Lead enrichment (2,000-10,000 records/mo depending on tier) identifies visitors before they even fill out a form. Every Leadpages plan also includes an MCP connector for publishing pages directly from Claude or ChatGPT -- a feature Systeme.io does not have.

Systeme.io does not have comparable CRO tooling -- its A/B testing is more basic, and it has no heatmaps, no AI traffic routing, and no lead enrichment. If your business model lives or dies on squeezing conversion rate out of a handful of high-traffic landing pages, that gap matters.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

Leadpages failure modes

  • No free plan, and the cheap tiers are a different product. The $8-39/mo HTML Pub tiers only publish pages -- if you want A/B testing or CRO tools, you need Grow at $79/mo annual minimum, a real price jump from the entry price advertised.
  • Zero email marketing, funnels, or courses at any price. You will be paying for and maintaining a second (or third) tool alongside Leadpages no matter which plan you are on.
  • Team seats do not appear until Optimize ($159/mo annual). Solo operators are fine, but a two-person team pays a real premium just to add a second login.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • No AI traffic routing, heatmaps, or lead enrichment. JUAN note here if you have tried Systeme.io built-in A/B testing and how it compares to a dedicated CRO tool
  • Landing-page design flexibility is more basic than a page-first specialist tool. Systeme.io page builder is good enough for funnels and sales pages, not built to compete with Leadpages on pure landing-page polish.
  • No MCP/AI-assistant publishing integration the way Leadpages ships on every plan.

Verdict by business type

  • Just starting out, need email + funnels + a course, tight or zero budget: Systeme.io free plan. Leadpages cannot touch this -- it has no equivalent free tier and does not do email/funnels/courses at any price.
  • Solo creator who already has email/funnels sorted and just wants the best landing pages: Leadpages Grow ($79/mo annual) for A/B testing and lead enrichment.
  • High-traffic paid-ads business obsessing over conversion rate: Leadpages Optimize or Scale ($159-319/mo annual) for Smart Traffic AI routing and heatmaps -- worth it once the traffic volume justifies the optimization spend.
  • Running the whole business (funnels, email, courses, community) out of one tool on a budget: Systeme.io Startup to Unlimited ($17-97/mo) -- still cheaper than Leadpages Grow alone, and it replaces 2-3 other tools.
  • Need automation beyond funnels or pages (AI agents, complex workflows): Neither -- see my n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than Leadpages?

Yes, and it also does far more for the price. Systeme.io has a permanent free plan (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, unlimited email). Leadpages has no free plan -- only a 7-day trial -- and its lowest tier with real CRO features (Grow: A/B testing, unlimited pages, unlimited traffic) is $99/mo ($79/mo annual). Leadpages cheaper HTML Pub tiers ($8-39/mo annual) only publish pages -- no A/B testing, no email marketing, no funnels, no courses.

Does Leadpages have a free plan?

No. Leadpages offers a 7-day free trial only, on any plan, then bills. Systeme.io has a permanent free plan with no credit card required and no expiration -- 2,000 contacts, 3 sales funnels, 1 course, 1 blog, unlimited email.

Which is better for a solo creator on a tight budget?

Systeme.io, if you need email marketing, sales funnels, or a course platform in the same tool -- Leadpages does not include any of those at any price, it is a landing-page and CRO specialist only. If you already own an email tool and a funnel builder and specifically want best-in-class A/B testing, heatmaps, and lead enrichment, Leadpages Grow or Optimize ($79-159/mo annual) is a focused tool worth the premium.
