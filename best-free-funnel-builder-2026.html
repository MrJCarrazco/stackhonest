Tool by tool

1. Systeme.io -- the only real free funnel builder left

The free plan is a complete, sellable funnel path: 3 sales funnels with up to 15 total steps, squeeze pages, order forms, 1-click upsell and order bump (one of each), a checkout with 0% platform transaction fees, unlimited email sends to 2,000 contacts, 1 automation rule, 1 workflow, 1 custom domain, and even a website and unlimited file storage. No credit card at signup, no expiry. I've run a live funnel on it -- the long version is in my Systeme.io review.

Where it breaks: one automation rule means exactly one trigger-action pair, one A/B test at a time, and the page editor is functional rather than beautiful -- if pixel-perfect design is the product, you'll feel the ceiling. Startup at $17/month lifts you to 10 funnels, 10 automation rules, 5,000 contacts, and 3 custom domains, which is still less than a fifth of ClickFunnels' entry price.

2. ClickFunnels -- the best-known name, and not free at any tier

Saying it plainly because the ads won't: ClickFunnels has no free plan. The path in is a free trial of Launch at $81/month billed annually ($97 month-to-month), which does include unlimited funnels, courses, email at 50K sends/month, and 5 custom domains -- a genuinely capable suite once you're paying. If you're deciding between the two as paid products, my Systeme.io vs ClickFunnels head-to-head walks the full feature ledger. For this article's question -- best free funnel builder -- it simply isn't eligible.

3. GetResponse -- funnels exist, but two paid tiers deep

GetResponse's conversion funnel feature is real and its email engine is strong, but the pricing page puts "Sales funnels" on the Marketer plan at $59/month ($48.38 annual) -- the $19/month Starter tier gets you a landing page builder, welcome series, and one automation workflow, not the funnel product. And no permanent free plan appears on the page at all. Decent value paid, wrong answer for free. If email automation is the actual job, see GetResponse vs Kit.

4. Kit -- free landing pages at scale, not a funnel builder

Kit's free plan gives you unlimited landing pages and forms, a 10,000-subscriber ceiling, and the ability to sell digital products off the free tier -- which covers the "collect emails, sell one thing" pattern many people actually mean when they type "funnel." What it doesn't have is funnel sequencing: no multi-step page flows, no order bumps or one-click upsells, and no automations on free (sequences need Creator, $33/month billed yearly). Newsletter-first businesses should read my free email marketing comparison -- Kit wins that fight decisively; it just isn't fighting in this one.

5. MailerLite -- fine free email, funnel-shaped only by squinting

MailerLite's free plan now caps at 250 subscribers with 1 landing page, 1 website, 3 forms, 3 automations, and 1 digital product. Those three free automations are more than Kit gives you, but one landing page and 250 contacts is a proof-of-concept allowance, not a funnel operation. Its real pitch is cheap paid email ($10.80/month annual for Comfort) -- covered properly in Kit vs MailerLite.
