Systeme.io vs Podia (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated September 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools, and I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: Systeme.io is the cheaper starting point -- its permanent free plan bundles course hosting, unlimited email sends, and a funnel builder in one place, with 0% transaction fees on every tier. Podia discontinued its free plan in 2024; every current tier is paid, starting at $42/mo billed annually ($49/mo monthly) on Mover, which still charges a 5% transaction fee on top of the subscription -- that fee only drops to 0% once you upgrade to Shaker at $84/mo annual. Podia's differentiator is a genuinely built-in community feature (spaces) bundled with courses, coaching, and email on every plan, something Systeme.io does not offer in the same form. If you are selling one course and want a $0 starting point with email and funnels included, Systeme.io wins on cost. If your business is community-first -- a paid membership space plus courses and coaching under one roof -- Podia's bundle is closer to purpose-built, once you accept there is no free tier anymore.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you want course hosting, email marketing, and a funnel builder bundled for free, and community/spaces is not central to your offer. Pick Podia if a paid community space alongside courses and coaching is the core product, and you are fine paying $42-84/mo annual (5% fee on the cheapest tier) since there is no free plan anymore.

The real fork in the road is what each platform is built around. Systeme.io is built around the funnel -- get a lead in, nurture by email, sell through a checkout -- with courses as one product type among several. Podia is built around the community -- a paid space where members show up repeatedly -- with courses, coaching, and digital downloads sold alongside that space. Both bundle email; only Podia bundles a real community feature, and only Systeme.io bundles a real funnel builder.

What Podia has that Systeme.io does not

Podia's community spaces are the standout: up to 25 spaces on the cheapest paid tier, scaling to unlimited on Earthquaker, positioned as genuinely core infrastructure rather than a bolted-on forum. It also includes live and in-person events, coaching product types, and 1,900+ integrations across every plan, plus migration help for moving existing products over. For a creator whose business model is "pay to be in the room" -- a membership community with courses and coaching layered in -- that is closer to purpose-built than Systeme.io's funnel-first approach.

The catch: none of that is free anymore. Podia's 2024 decision to kill its free tier means every creator starts at a minimum $42/mo commitment (or a ticking 30-day trial clock), and the cheapest tier's 5% transaction fee plus Stripe/PayPal's own 2.9%+30 cents stacks up fast -- a $50 sale on Mover nets roughly $4 in combined fees before you keep anything.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

Podia failure modes

  • No free plan since 2024. Every path now runs through a 30-day trial that converts to a paid plan or your site stops being available -- no permanent free fallback like Systeme.io.
  • The Mover tier's 5% fee compounds with processor fees fast. At $1,000/mo in sales, Mover and Shaker cost roughly the same once the fee is counted -- below that Mover is cheaper, above it the 5% cut keeps climbing while Shaker stays flat.
  • Email subscriber caps are tight relative to the price. 100 subscribers on a $42/mo plan is a low ceiling -- a modest list forces an upgrade to Shaker just for headroom, separate from any feature need.
  • Affiliate marketing is Shaker-and-up only. Mover customers cannot run an affiliate program for their own products at all, unlike Systeme.io which includes affiliate tooling from the free plan.
  • No dedicated multi-step funnel builder. Podia's sales pages and checkout are solid but not built for multi-page funnel sequences the way Systeme.io is.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • No dedicated community/spaces feature. Systeme.io has no equivalent to Podia's community spaces -- if membership community is your core offer, you would be working around the tool, not with it.
  • No live/in-person events tooling and no native coaching product type in the way Podia packages them explicitly.

Verdict by business type

  • Selling one course plus building an email list and a sales funnel: Systeme.io free plan -- the whole stack (funnel, email, course) at $0, versus Podia's $42/mo-plus-5%-fee starting point with a tight 100-subscriber cap.
  • Community-first business -- paid membership space with courses/coaching layered in: Podia Shaker ($84/mo annual) -- the community spaces and 0% fee at that tier are the closest fit, once you accept there is no free option.
  • Validating a first course with uncertain sales volume: Systeme.io free plan -- no transaction fee risk and no trial-expiry deadline while you find out if the course sells.
  • Need automation beyond course/funnel/email (custom workflows, API-driven agents): Neither -- see the n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than Podia?

Yes. Systeme.io has a permanent free plan that includes course hosting alongside funnels and email, with 0% transaction fees on every tier. Podia discontinued its free plan in 2024 -- every tier is now paid, starting at $42/mo billed annually ($49/mo monthly), and that cheapest Mover tier still charges a 5% transaction fee on every sale. The 0% Podia fee only starts at the Shaker plan, $84/mo annual.

Does Podia have a free plan in 2026?

No. Podia discontinued its free plan in 2024 and replaced it with a 30-day free trial that requires no credit card but converts to a paid plan afterward. The current lineup is Mover ($42/mo annual, 5% fee), Shaker ($84/mo annual, 0% fee), and Earthquaker ($150/mo annual, 0% fee).

Which one is better for a creator who also needs email and funnels?

Systeme.io, if budget matters more than community-first polish. Podia includes email marketing on every plan but with tight subscriber caps (100 on Mover, 500 on Shaker) and no dedicated funnel/landing-page builder comparable to Systeme.io. Systeme.io bundles course hosting, unlimited email sends, and a funnel builder into the same free plan, so a creator selling one course pays $0 on Systeme.io versus a minimum of $42/mo plus 5% per sale on Podia.
