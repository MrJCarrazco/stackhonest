How to Build a SaaS Landing Page with Systeme.io (2026 Guide)

Last updated September 2026 · ~8 min read · by Juan

Short answer: Systeme.io free plan (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 custom domain, 0% transaction fees, no credit card required) is enough to build a real SaaS landing page with an email-capture form, pricing table, and hosted checkout -- in one afternoon, no code. Sign up, pick the Sales funnel template, swap in your copy and pricing, connect your domain, publish. You outgrow the free plan only when you pass 2,000 contacts or need more than 3 funnels.

What you need before you start

  • A Systeme.io account (free plan, no credit card).
  • Your SaaS pricing decided -- at least a free trial or one paid tier. You cannot write a good landing page around pricing you have not committed to.
  • A domain if you want yourname.com instead of a systeme.io subdomain. The free plan supports 1 custom domain -- you just need to own it (Namecheap, Cloudflare, wherever) and point its DNS at Systeme.io.
  • Stripe or PayPal account if you are taking payment on this page rather than just capturing emails for a waitlist.

Step-by-step

1. Create the funnel

Inside Systeme.io, go to Sales Funnels → Create. Pick a template close to what you need -- Squeeze page if you are just building a waitlist/email-capture page, Sales page if you are selling a subscription directly. The free plan gives you 3 funnels total, so build one funnel with a landing step and (optionally) a thank-you/upsell step rather than three separate one-off pages.

2. Write the page in the block editor

The editor is drag-and-drop blocks (headline, image, video, pricing table, countdown timer, form). For a SaaS landing page the block order that converts best in my experience: headline stating the outcome (not the feature) → 30-second demo video or GIF → 3-column pricing table → FAQ block → final CTA form. 3. Connect email capture to your list

Drop a form block tied to your Systeme.io contact list (unlimited email sends on every plan, including free). Anyone who submits gets added automatically -- no separate email tool needed for the first 2,000 contacts.

4. Wire up payment (if selling direct)

If you are selling a subscription rather than just capturing leads, add a Stripe or PayPal integration under Payment settings and place a checkout block on the page. Systeme.io charges 0% transaction fees on every plan -- Stripe/PayPal still take their own standard processing cut, that is unavoidable anywhere.

5. Connect your domain and publish

Under Settings → Domains, add your custom domain and update its DNS records (Systeme.io gives you the exact A record / CNAME to add at your registrar). DNS propagation usually takes under an hour. Until then the page is live on your free systeme.io subdomain, so you can test everything before the custom domain kicks in.

Common failure points

  • Publishing before the domain propagates and thinking it is broken. Check the subdomain URL first -- if that works, it is a DNS timing issue, not a Systeme.io problem.
  • Trying to cram a full multi-page marketing site into 1 funnel. The free plan's 3-funnel cap is generous for a single landing page + thank-you page, but do not try to build your whole site's navigation inside one funnel step.
  • No pricing decided before writing copy. A landing page without committed pricing reads vague. Decide pricing first, write copy second.
  • Forgetting the affiliate program setup if you plan to run one. Systeme.io includes a built-in affiliate program on every plan -- worth turning on early even before you have affiliates, since backfilling it later means rebuilding tracking links into already-published pages.

Where the free plan runs out

Verified against systeme.io/pricing, September 2026: the free plan covers 2,000 contacts, 3 sales funnels, 1 blog, 1 course, 1 affiliate program, 1 custom domain, unlimited email sends and file storage, and 0% transaction fees -- permanently, no credit card required. You need to upgrade when you cross 2,000 contacts (Startup, $17/mo: 5,000 contacts, unlimited funnels, 5 courses) or need more than 3 funnels for additional products/campaigns. For most single-product SaaS landing pages, the free plan alone gets you through your entire pre-launch waitlist phase.

The tools used

Everything in this guide runs on Systeme.io's free plan -- funnel builder, email capture, custom domain, and (if you add Stripe/PayPal) checkout. No separate landing-page tool, no separate email tool, no separate affiliate-tracking tool needed until you outgrow 2,000 contacts.

FAQ

Can I build a SaaS landing page for free with Systeme.io?

Yes. Systeme.io free plan includes 3 sales funnels, 1 custom domain, unlimited email sends, and 0% transaction fees -- enough to build and host a full SaaS landing page with an email-capture form and a pricing section, no code required.

Do I need a developer to build this?

No. Systeme.io funnel builder is drag-and-drop with pre-built templates. You will need basic copywriting and your own domain if you want a custom URL instead of the free systeme.io subdomain.

When do I outgrow the free plan?

When you pass 2,000 contacts or need more than 3 funnels or 1 course. At that point Startup ($17/mo) covers 5,000 contacts and unlimited funnels -- still far cheaper than a ClickFunnels + Mailchimp + Teachable stack.
