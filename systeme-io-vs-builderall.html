Systeme.io vs Builderall (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated September 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools, and I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: Systeme.io has a permanent free plan (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course, unlimited email, 0% transaction fees) that costs nothing indefinitely and never requires a card. Builderall has no free plan in 2026 -- its cheapest tier, Core, runs $27/mo monthly ($12.75/mo billed annually) and does not include email marketing (MailingBoss/email automation only shows up starting at Essentials, $47/mo or $35.25/mo annual). Builderall's pitch is breadth: 25+ bundled tools including a "Supercharge" package, AI credits, and a full course/video hosting suite once you are on a paid tier, versus Systeme.io's tighter but genuinely free starting point. If you want to validate a business at $0 with funnels, email, and a course all included, Systeme.io wins outright. If you specifically need Builderall's wider tool bundle (its own AI image toolkit, dedicated Supercharge add-ons) and are ready to pay from day one, Builderall is the broader kit -- but you are paying at least $27/mo just to find out if it fits.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you want funnels, email, and course hosting bundled for free with 0% transaction fees, and you are not ready to commit a monthly budget yet. Pick Builderall if you specifically want its wider 25+ tool bundle (AI image credits, video hosting, multiple mailboxes) and are fine paying $27-247/mo from the start since there is no permanent free tier.

The fork here is scope versus cost. Builderall bundles a much wider tool count -- website builder, funnels, email (once you are past Core), AI image credits, video hosting, chatbots, and more -- inside one subscription, and independent trackers list it as covering 25+ marketing tools. What Builderall has that Systeme.io does not

Builderall's biggest differentiator is raw tool count: 25+ marketing tools bundled per plan, a dedicated AI image toolkit with credits scaling by tier (200 on Core up to much higher on Premium), a chatbot builder, and video hosting alongside its funnel/website builder. Higher tiers add multiple team seats (3 on Advanced, 10 on Premium) and dedicated VIP support with a free migration service on Premium. For an agency or a business already running several separate tools, consolidating into Builderall's single subscription could replace multiple bills.

The catch: none of that is available to test without paying, beyond a 14-day trial. And email marketing -- arguably the most commonly needed feature for anyone running funnels -- is not included on the entry Core tier at all, so the realistic starting price for a "complete" Builderall stack is Essentials at $47/mo ($35.25/mo annual), not the advertised $27/mo Core price.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

Builderall failure modes

  • No permanent free plan. Every path runs through a 14-day trial or a paid-then-refunded 30-day window -- there is no indefinite $0 tier like Systeme.io to fall back on while validating an idea.
  • Email marketing is not on the entry tier. Core ($27/mo) excludes MailingBoss on most current listings, meaning the real floor for a working funnel-plus-email stack is Essentials at $47/mo -- the same price as Systeme.io's Webinar tier, which includes far more.
  • Storage is thin even on paid tiers. 1GB on Core and Essentials is a tight ceiling for a site with video or image-heavy pages.
  • Reported reliability and support-depth complaints. Independent review aggregation across Trustpilot, G2, and Capterra shows a consistent pattern of occasional outages and support response times stretching beyond a day on complex issues.
  • Interface density. With 25+ bundled tools, new users report the settings and module sprawl take real time to learn before the platform pays for itself.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • Fewer bundled tool categories. No dedicated AI image toolkit, no chatbot builder, no video hosting comparable to Builderall's -- Systeme.io is funnel/email/course focused, not an everything-bundle.
  • Visual polish is more basic. Funnels and pages are functional rather than distinctive compared to more design-forward builders.

Verdict by business type

  • Validating a first offer with zero budget: Systeme.io free plan -- funnels, email, and one course at $0 indefinitely, no trial clock ticking.
  • Agency or business already paying for 4-5 separate marketing tools: Builderall Advanced ($97/mo, $72.75/mo annual) -- if consolidating a website builder, email tool, AI image tool, and video host into one bill genuinely replaces what you are paying elsewhere, the breadth pays for itself.
  • Need email marketing included from the cheapest possible entry price: Systeme.io -- included free at 2,000 contacts, versus Builderall requiring the $47/mo Essentials tier just to get MailingBoss.
  • Need automation beyond funnel/email/course (custom workflows, API-driven agents): Neither -- see the n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than Builderall?

Yes, for anyone starting out. Systeme.io has a permanent free plan (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course, unlimited email, 0% transaction fees) that costs nothing indefinitely. Builderall has no free plan at all in 2026 -- its cheapest tier, Core, is $27/mo monthly or $12.75/mo billed annually, and it does not include email marketing (MailingBoss is bundled starting at the Essentials tier, $47/mo or $35.25/mo annual).

Does Builderall have a free trial in 2026?

Yes, most listings show a 14-day free trial, no credit card required for some promotions, plus a 30-day money-back guarantee once you do subscribe. There is no permanent free tier -- after the trial or refund window, every plan requires payment. Systeme.io's free plan, by contrast, never expires and never requires a card.

Which one is better for someone who needs email marketing included from day one?

Systeme.io. Its free plan includes unlimited email sends, automation, and tagging with no subscriber-based paywall until 2,000 contacts. Builderall's Core plan ($27/mo) does not include MailingBoss email marketing on most current listings -- you need Essentials ($47/mo) or higher to get email automation, subscriber management, and meaningful send volume (50,000/mo).
