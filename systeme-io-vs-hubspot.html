Systeme.io vs HubSpot (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated October 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools, and I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: Systeme.io is the better fit for solopreneurs and small businesses running funnels, email, and courses -- its $0-97/mo plans include all three with 0% transaction fees and zero onboarding cost. HubSpot Free tier is genuinely free and includes a real CRM, but its marketing tools cap at 1,000 marketing contacts and 2,000 emails a month with HubSpot branding baked into everything. The honest middle tier, Starter, runs $9-20 per seat per month (pricing fluctuates by promo) -- reasonable, but still no courses, no funnels, no affiliate program. The real wall is Marketing Hub Professional at $800/mo (annual, 3 seats, 2,000 contacts) plus a mandatory one-time $3,000 onboarding fee. If you need HubSpot CRM depth and sales-pipeline tooling for a larger team, that cost can make sense. If you are a solo creator or small business selling through funnels and courses, Systeme.io covers more of what you actually need for a fraction of the cost.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you want funnels, email, and course hosting bundled together without an enterprise sales process. Pick HubSpot if you need serious CRM and sales-pipeline depth for a growing team and can absorb $800-3,600/mo plus a $3,000-7,000 onboarding fee once you outgrow the free and Starter tiers.

The fork here is simple: Systeme.io is built for a solo creator or small business running the whole funnel-to-sale motion themselves. HubSpot is built for a team that needs real CRM infrastructure and can pay enterprise prices once they outgrow the free tier -- the jump from Starter to Professional is not incremental, it is an order of magnitude (roughly $20/mo/seat to $800/mo plus a $3,000 one-time fee).

What HubSpot has that Systeme.io does not

HubSpot CRM is the real differentiator -- deal pipelines, contact timelines, deep reporting, and an ecosystem of 2,000+ app integrations that Systeme.io does not attempt to match. For a sales team tracking complex, multi-touch B2B deals, HubSpot Sales Hub and CRM segment data are purpose-built in a way no all-in-one funnel tool replicates. HubSpot also offers AI agents (Agent Hub, Content Agent, Customer Agent) bundled into paid tiers that automate parts of lead generation and customer service, credit-metered rather than flat-rate.

The catch: none of that CRM depth matters if you are a solo creator selling a course or running an email list. HubSpot does not include course hosting or an affiliate program at any price point -- you would need separate tools (and separate subscriptions) to cover what Systeme.io bundles into its free plan.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

HubSpot failure modes

  • The Starter-to-Professional jump is brutal. Starter tops out around $20/mo/seat; Professional starts at $800/mo for 3 seats plus a mandatory $3,000 one-time onboarding fee -- there is no gentle middle tier for a growing small business.
  • No course hosting or affiliate program at any tier. If your business model includes digital products or an affiliate program, HubSpot simply does not do it -- you are buying and integrating separate tools.
  • Free and Starter tiers brand everything. Emails, live chat, and forms carry HubSpot branding until Starter (chat/forms) or Professional (full removal in some areas) -- a real constraint for anyone building their own brand.
  • HubSpot Credits meter AI features separately. Beyond the subscription, AI agent actions consume credits you buy and manage on top of your plan cost -- an extra line item Systeme.io does not have.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • CRM and sales-pipeline tooling is basic. Systeme.io pipeline feature is a simple deal tracker, nowhere near HubSpot segment-based CRM depth -- a complex B2B sales process will outgrow it.
  • No dedicated AI agent suite. Systeme.io does not offer anything comparable to HubSpot Agent Hub or Content Agent for automating lead qualification or support conversations.
  • Smaller third-party integration ecosystem. HubSpot 2,000+ app marketplace dwarfs what Systeme.io connects to natively.

Verdict by business type

  • Solo creator selling a course, running a funnel and email list: Systeme.io free plan -- the whole stack at $0, versus HubSpot branded, course-less free tier.
  • Small team needing real CRM and deal-pipeline tracking, budget under $20/mo/seat: HubSpot Starter -- removes branding, adds simple automation, still affordable.
  • Growing B2B sales team needing omni-channel marketing automation and can absorb enterprise pricing: HubSpot Professional -- the $800/mo plus $3,000 onboarding fee buys genuine CRM and automation depth Systeme.io does not attempt.
  • Need workflow automation beyond CRM/funnel/email (custom API-driven agents): Neither -- see the n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than HubSpot?

Yes, once you need marketing automation beyond a basic free CRM. HubSpot Free plan genuinely has no cost, but its marketing tools cap at 1,000 marketing contacts and 2,000 emails a month with HubSpot branding on everything. The first real paid marketing tier, Starter, runs $9-20 per seat per month depending on billing. The serious jump is Marketing Hub Professional at $800/mo (annual, 3 seats, 2,000 contacts) plus a mandatory one-time $3,000 onboarding fee. Systeme.io covers funnels, email, and course hosting for $0-97/mo with zero onboarding fee at any tier.

Does HubSpot charge an onboarding fee?

Yes, for Professional and Enterprise tiers. Marketing Hub Professional requires a one-time $3,000 Professional Onboarding fee on top of the $800 monthly subscription. Enterprise requires a $7,000 onboarding fee on top of $3,600 a month. The Free and Starter tiers do not require onboarding fees. Systeme.io has never charged an onboarding fee at any tier.

Which one is better for a solopreneur selling courses or running funnels?

Systeme.io. HubSpot does not include course hosting or an affiliate program at any tier, and its free plan 1,000-contact, 2,000-email ceiling with mandatory branding is tight for anyone trying to run real campaigns. Systeme.io free plan bundles 2,000 contacts, unlimited email sends, 1 course, and 3 funnels with 0% transaction fees and no branding requirement. HubSpot strength is CRM depth and sales-pipeline tooling for larger teams, not solo marketing-and-sales workflows.
