Systeme.io vs Mailchimp (2026): Which One Actually Saves You Money

Last updated September 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools -- I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: Systeme.io free plan (2,000 contacts, unlimited email, 0% fees) already beats Mailchimp paid Essentials tier ($13/mo, 500 contacts) on both contact count and price -- and Systeme.io throws in funnels, a course platform, and an affiliate program at no extra cost. Mailchimp is email-only but more specialized: deeper deliverability tooling, more integrations, and predictive segmentation on its higher tiers. If email is genuinely all you need and you have the budget, Mailchimp can still make sense. For everyone else, Systeme.io is the cheaper, broader tool.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you want email plus funnels, courses, and an affiliate program bundled into one price, or if you are starting with zero budget. Pick Mailchimp if email deliverability and sophisticated segmentation/automation are your single priority, you already have funnels and course delivery handled elsewhere, and you can absorb Mailchimp per-contact pricing curve as your list grows.

The free-tier gap is the sharpest part of this comparison: Systeme.io free plan holds 8x the contacts of Mailchimp free plan (2,000 vs 250) and does not throttle you to 500 sends a month. To match Systeme.io free-tier contact count on Mailchimp you would already be paying for Essentials or Standard, and you would still only be getting email -- no funnels, no course platform, no affiliate program.

What Mailchimp has that Systeme.io does not

Mailchimp is a dedicated email platform with a decade-plus head start on deliverability infrastructure, and it shows in the feature depth: predictive segmentation (Standard/Premium), up to 200 automation flow steps, 300+ third-party integrations, and popup forms with A/B/multivariate testing on the higher tiers. If your business is pure e-commerce or content-newsletter and you already have a separate funnel/checkout/course tool, Mailchimp specialization in that one lane can outperform Systeme.io more generalist email tooling.

Systeme.io does not try to compete on deliverability sophistication or third-party integration breadth -- it wins by bundling email with the rest of the funnel stack instead of making you stitch together 3-4 separate tools (see my Systeme.io vs Kartra comparison for the same bundling argument against a different competitor).

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

Mailchimp failure modes

  • The free plan is nearly unusable for real growth. 250 contacts and a hard cap of 500 sends/month (or 250/day) means most solo creators outgrow it within weeks, not months.
  • Pricing scales aggressively with list size. Premium starts at $350/mo for the same 500-contact baseline as Essentials/Standard -- the jump exists specifically to gate advanced features behind large lists, so a small list wanting Premium features still pays the $350 floor.
  • No funnels, courses, or affiliate tooling. You will be paying for and maintaining separate tools for anything beyond sending email.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • Email deliverability tooling is thinner. [JUAN: note here if you have run into deliverability issues on Systeme.io vs a dedicated ESP and how you resolved it]
  • Fewer third-party integrations than Mailchimp 300+. If your stack depends on niche integrations, check Systeme.io list before committing.
  • Automation depth is more basic than Mailchimp Standard/Premium flow-builder, which supports up to 200 automation flows against Systeme.io simpler rule-based automations.

Verdict by business type

  • Solo creator or small business just starting out: Systeme.io free plan. 2,000 contacts and unlimited sends beats Mailchimp 250-contact free tier by a wide margin, and you get funnels and a course platform thrown in.
  • Running an online course or membership on a budget: Systeme.io Startup ($17/mo) -- Mailchimp has no course or membership tooling at any price.
  • Pure e-commerce or newsletter business with existing funnel/checkout tooling: Mailchimp Standard ($20/mo+) if predictive segmentation and deep integrations matter more to you than bundling.
  • Large list (10,000+) needing dedicated support and advanced deliverability: Mailchimp Premium, but confirm the $350/mo floor is worth it versus Systeme.io Unlimited ($97/mo) for unlimited contacts across the whole stack.
  • Need automation beyond email (AI agents, complex workflows): Neither -- see my n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than Mailchimp?

Yes, at almost every contact tier. Systeme.io free plan covers 2,000 contacts with unlimited email sends and 0% transaction fees. Mailchimp free plan caps out at just 250 contacts and 500 sends/month, then jumps to $13/mo (Essentials) for 500 contacts -- and Mailchimp is email-only, while Systeme.io free plan also includes funnels, a course, and an affiliate program.

What does Mailchimp have that Systeme.io does not?

Mailchimp is a more mature, dedicated email-marketing platform: deeper deliverability tooling, more third-party integrations (300+), predictive segmentation on Standard/Premium, and broader brand recognition. If email deliverability at scale is your single biggest concern and you do not need funnels or courses, Mailchimp specialization can be worth the higher price.

Does Mailchimp charge transaction fees?

No, Mailchimp does not charge per-transaction fees -- it charges by contact count and monthly email sends instead. Systeme.io also charges 0% transaction fees on every plan including free, but bundles funnels, courses, and affiliate management into the same price where Mailchimp is email-only.
