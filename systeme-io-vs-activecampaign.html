Systeme.io vs ActiveCampaign (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated October 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools, and I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: these two tools are not really competing for the same buyer. Systeme.io is a funnel-and-course platform with email built in -- $0-97/mo flat, 0% transaction fees, same price regardless of contact count within a plan limit. ActiveCampaign is an email-marketing-and-automation platform with a CRM built in -- genuinely best-in-class deliverability and automation logic, but no funnel builder, no checkout, and no course hosting at any tier. Pricing starts at $15/mo for Starter (1,000 contacts, billed annually) and climbs with both plan tier and contact count: Plus $49/mo, Pro $79/mo, Enterprise $145/mo, all quoted at the 1,000-contact level and all requiring annual billing to hit that price. If you are selling a product through a funnel or hosting a course, Systeme.io covers that from its free plan. If you already have a list and need sophisticated segmentation, predictive sending, and conditional content, ActiveCampaign automation engine is the real deal -- you will just be paying separately for whatever sells the thing.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you need funnels, checkout, and course hosting bundled with email, and you want a flat price that does not climb with your contact count. Pick ActiveCampaign if you already have an audience and need serious automation sophistication -- predictive sending, conditional content, premium CRM integrations -- and you are fine paying separately for whatever actually sells your product.

The honest framing: these solve different problems. Systeme.io is the everything-to-sell-one-thing tool. ActiveCampaign is the email-like-a-grown-up-once-you-already-have-an-audience-and-a-sales-mechanism-elsewhere tool. Comparing them head-to-head only makes sense if you are deciding which single tool to start with -- and for most solo creators and small businesses just getting off the ground, that is Systeme.io, because it is the only one of the two that includes a way to actually sell something.

What ActiveCampaign has that Systeme.io does not

ActiveCampaign automation engine is genuinely more sophisticated once you are past Systeme.io basic rule-and-workflow system. Predictive sending (send-time optimization per contact), conditional content blocks inside a single email template, premium segmentation across many behavioral signals, and Active Intelligence AI features (AI campaign builder, AI automation builder) on the higher tiers are all real differentiators for a list-heavy business running complex, multi-touch nurture sequences. The CRM also goes deeper on Pro and Enterprise, with premium integrations into Salesforce and other sales tools Systeme.io does not attempt to match.

The catch: none of that matters if you do not already have a product, a funnel, and a way to collect payment. ActiveCampaign assumes those exist elsewhere.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

ActiveCampaign failure modes

  • No funnel, checkout, or course builder at any price. You are buying a second (and often third) tool no matter which ActiveCampaign tier you land on, if your business model involves selling a digital product or course directly.
  • Pricing scales on two axes at once. Both the plan tier AND your contact count push the price up -- a list that grows from 1,000 to 10,000 contacts multiplies cost even if you never upgrade tiers.
  • The quoted price requires annual billing. Starter $15/mo and every other tier headline number only apply billed annually; month-to-month runs meaningfully higher, a common ESP pattern that is easy to miss at signup.
  • Starter automation cap is tight. 5 actions per automation on the entry tier is a real ceiling for anything beyond a simple welcome sequence.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • Automation logic is basic by comparison. No predictive sending, no conditional content blocks, no premium behavioral segmentation -- a list-heavy business with complex nurture logic will outgrow Systeme.io automation rules.
  • No dedicated AI campaign/automation builder. Systeme.io does not have anything comparable to ActiveCampaign Active Intelligence suite.
  • CRM is a simple pipeline tool. Fine for tracking a handful of deals, nowhere near ActiveCampaign premium CRM integrations for a sales team.

Verdict by business type

  • Solo creator or small business selling a course, digital product, or service through a funnel: Systeme.io free plan -- the whole sell-and-deliver stack at $0, no separate checkout tool needed.
  • Established list with a sales mechanism already in place, needing sophisticated automation and segmentation: ActiveCampaign Plus or Pro -- the automation depth is the real product here.
  • Agency or sales team needing premium CRM integrations, SSO, and dedicated support: ActiveCampaign Enterprise -- $145/mo+ buys infrastructure Systeme.io does not attempt.
  • Need workflow automation beyond CRM/email (custom API-driven agents): Neither -- see the n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than ActiveCampaign?

Yes, for most solo creators and small businesses. ActiveCampaign has no free plan -- its Starter tier runs $15/mo billed annually for 1,000 contacts, and that price climbs as your contact count grows, regardless of which plan you pick. Plus is $49/mo, Pro is $79/mo, Enterprise is $145/mo, all at the 1,000-contact tier and all requiring annual billing to get the quoted price. Systeme.io four plans are flat at $0, $17, $47, and $97/mo regardless of billing term, and include funnels, email, and course hosting at every tier with 0% transaction fees.

Does ActiveCampaign include funnels or course hosting?

No. ActiveCampaign is an email marketing and automation platform with a built-in CRM -- it has landing pages starting on the Plus plan, but no multi-step sales funnel builder, no checkout/payment processing, and no course hosting at any tier. You would need separate tools for those, which Systeme.io bundles in from its free plan.

Which one is better for a solo creator selling a course or digital product?

Systeme.io. Its free plan already includes a funnel builder, checkout with 0% transaction fees, 1 course, and unlimited email sends. ActiveCampaign strength is deliverability and automation sophistication for an existing list -- genuinely best-in-class there -- but you would still need a separate funnel and checkout tool (and a separate course host) to run the same business, stacking subscriptions on top of ActiveCampaign per-contact pricing.
