1. n8n — the one I run my own business on

What the listicles won't tell you: n8n's pricing model is the whole story. Zapier charges per task (every step of every run). n8n cloud charges per execution (one run = one execution, no matter if it's 3 steps or 300). A 50-step AI agent workflow that runs 1,000 times a month is a rounding error on n8n and a car payment on Zapier.

Where it breaks: self-hosting means you're the sysadmin. Version upgrades occasionally break community nodes. Error handling is on you — build an error-trigger workflow on day one (it's in my free template pack).

Who should skip it: if the word "webhook" makes your eyes glaze over and you don't want that to change, use Make.

2. Make — best for non-technical operators

Make's visual canvas is genuinely the easiest way to see what an automation does. 1,000 free operations a month is enough to run a real small-business workflow (a lead router, an invoice chaser). Where it breaks: loops and branching logic get visually unmanageable past ~20 modules, and debugging a failed scenario mid-run is worse than n8n's execution log.

3. Zapier — fine, but check the math first

Zapier has the most app integrations and the least friction. But per-task pricing means costs scale with success: the busier your automation gets, the more you pay per step. I moved everything off Zapier and my automation bill dropped ~90%. If your team is non-technical and the budget is real, it's still a defensible pick. (Fun fact: Zapier doesn't even have an affiliate program — every "Zapier is #1!" listicle you've read placed it there for free. Draw your own conclusions about listicles.)
