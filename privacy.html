Privacy

Updated October 16, 2026. StackHonest is published by Digital Consulting Studios. This notice describes the current static website, not the websites linked from it.

Browsing this site

The current StackHonest pages contain no analytics or advertising scripts, and no code that sets cookies or uses browser storage. There is no account, comment box or active newsletter signup. That does not make a visit anonymous to the hosting provider: your browser still sends requests to GitHub Pages to load the site.

GitHub operates the hosting infrastructure and describes its own data handling in its privacy statement. StackHonest does not control the host's processing or retention.

Links and affiliate attribution

Some outbound links contain affiliate identifiers. If you follow one, the destination or affiliate provider may process your visit and use its own cookies or other tracking under its own privacy notice. The affiliate identifier in a link identifies this publisher; you do not need to provide an email address to browse StackHonest. Browser privacy controls can limit cookies and referrer information. This site does not implement a separate response to Do Not Track signals.

If you email the studio

For corrections or privacy questions, email digitalconsultingstudios@gmail.com. Include the relevant page address and only the information needed to explain the request; do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents. Email provides your sender address and whatever message content you choose to send.

The inbox is hosted by Gmail; Google's privacy policy describes that provider's processing. Messages are used to handle the inquiry, not to enroll you in a newsletter. No fixed retention deadline is promised here; retention can depend on the inquiry and the provider's systems. You can use the same address to request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied; any applicable retention obligations and the email provider's systems may limit what can be removed.

Changes to this notice

The dated notice on this page will be updated if the site's data collection changes. Email collection or a new analytics integration must not be enabled without updating this notice and checking the actual delivery and consent flow.
