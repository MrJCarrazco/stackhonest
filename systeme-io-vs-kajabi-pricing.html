Systeme.io vs Kajabi (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated September 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Short answer: Systeme.io is dramatically cheaper and has a real permanent free plan. Kajabi has no free plan at all -- and after its January 13, 2026 price restructure, the cheapest way in is Basic at $179 a month ($143 a month annual) for just 2,500 contacts and 5 products. Kajabi also eliminated its old $89 a month Kickstarter tier entirely, so anyone quoting Kajabi at under $179 a month is quoting dead pricing. If budget is the deciding factor, Systeme.io wins outright. Kajabi case is a more polished all-in-one built specifically for course and coaching businesses with deeper community and membership tooling -- but you now pay a real premium for it.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you want a genuinely free plan, zero transaction fees with no surcharge tricks, and an all-in-one that does not punish you for growing your contact list. Pick Kajabi if you are building a serious course or coaching brand, want polished community and membership tooling, and have $179+ a month to spend before you have made a single sale.

What Kajabi has that Systeme.io does not

Kajabi is not just a pricier Systeme.io -- it is built specifically for the course and coaching niche and it shows. Communities, cohort-based courses, an assessments and quiz builder, and a polished mobile app for students all come standard from Basic up. Growth and Pro unlock advanced marketing automations and an affiliate program comparable to what Systeme.io includes on every tier. Kajabi University (its own training content on how to run a course business) is bundled at every level, and Pro adds a full code editor for deep customization. If you are running a serious 6-figure coaching or course brand and the $179-499 a month range is a rounding error against your revenue, that polish is the real pitch.

The catch: Kajabi own-Stripe surcharge (new in the January 2026 restructure) means the advertised zero-percent transaction fee only applies if you route every sale through Kajabi Payments rather than your own existing Stripe account -- bring your own Stripe and you eat an extra 0.5-2% on top of Stripe standard rate, depending on plan. Systeme.io charges 0% with no such condition, on every plan including free.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

Kajabi failure modes

  • No free plan, and the January 2026 hike doubled the effective entry price. The $89 a month Kickstarter tier is gone; Basic starts at $179 a month with a contact cap cut from a prior 10,000 down to 2,500, per multiple independent 2026 pricing trackers.
  • The own-Stripe surcharge is easy to miss. Advertised zero-percent transaction fees only hold inside Kajabi Payments. Bring your own Stripe account and Basic effectively costs you about 4.9% plus $0.30 per sale, not 2.9%.
  • Email sends are capped on lower tiers. Basic limits marketing emails per month; hit the cap mid-launch and you cannot send again until the next cycle -- exactly when you need to.
  • Forced upgrades, no overage option. Cross the Basic 2,500-contact ceiling and your only options are upgrade to Growth (plus $70 a month) or start deleting contacts.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • No dedicated community or cohort-course tooling to match Kajabi depth. Systeme.io includes 1 course on the free plan and more on paid tiers, but Kajabi cohort features and student mobile app are more built-out for a pure course business. Verdict by business type

  • Just starting out, tight or zero budget: Systeme.io free plan. Nothing to lose, and there is no equivalent free option on Kajabi at all.
  • Course creator or coach on a budget: Systeme.io Startup ($17/mo) -- 5,000 contacts and 0% fees for a tenth of Kajabi Basic.
  • Serious 6-figure coaching or course business, budget is not the constraint: Kajabi Growth or Pro ($249-499/mo) for the community and cohort tooling and polish, if you can absorb the January 2026 price hike.
  • Scaling contacts without paying a per-tier premium: Systeme.io Unlimited ($97/mo) covers unlimited contacts -- Kajabi Pro at $499/mo still caps you at 100,000.
  • Need automation beyond funnels (AI agents, complex workflows): Neither -- see my n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than Kajabi?

Yes, by a wide margin. Systeme.io has a permanent free plan (2,000 contacts, 0% fees). Kajabi has no free plan -- its cheapest tier (Basic) costs $179 a month monthly ($143 a month annual) for just 2,500 contacts and 5 products, after Kajabi January 2026 price hike eliminated the old $89 a month Kickstarter plan and doubled Basic from $89 to $179.

Did Kajabi pricing change in 2026?

Yes. Effective January 13, 2026, Kajabi eliminated its $89 a month Kickstarter plan entirely, raised Basic from $149 a month to $179 a month (a 100%+ jump for former Kickstarter users pushed onto Basic), raised Growth from $199 a month to $249 a month, and raised Pro from $399 a month to $499 a month -- with no grandfathered pricing for existing customers.

Does Kajabi really have 0% transaction fees?

Only if you route payments through Kajabi Payments, its own Stripe-backed processor. If you connect your own separate Stripe account instead, Kajabi charges an added surcharge on top of Stripe standard rate: 2% on Basic, 1% on Growth, 0.5% on Pro. On a Basic plan that pushes your real per-sale cost to roughly 4.9% plus $0.30, not the advertised 0%. Systeme.io charges 0% transaction fees on every plan with no such surcharge.
