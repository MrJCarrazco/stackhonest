The only real cost is your own time building an audience or content that sends people to your link.

Step by step

  1. Create a free Systeme.io account. Use this link or go to systeme.io directly, either way you land in your own dashboard within a couple minutes.
  2. Open your affiliate dashboard. It lives at systeme.io/dashboard/affiliate-dashboard. Your unique affiliate ID is generated automatically, there is no separate signup form or approval wait.
  3. Build your affiliate link. Append ?sa=YOURID to any systeme.io URL, the homepage, a pricing page, a specific plan. [JUAN: drop your actual dashboard screenshot here once available]
  4. Always drop the www. Systeme.io own help docs flag this: use https://systeme.io?sa=... not https://www.systeme.io?sa=..., the www version breaks tracking.
  5. Set up your payout method. PayPal or wire transfer, configured in the dashboard before your first payout cycle (processed the 10th of each month, $30 minimum).
  6. Share it wherever your audience already is. Blog posts, YouTube descriptions, email newsletters, once someone clicks and creates a free account, they are tagged to you for life.

Common failure points

  • Using the www version of the link. This is the single most common mistake per Systeme.io own help documentation, it silently breaks attribution, and you will not find out until you notice a referral you expected never got credited.
  • Assuming a click alone counts. It does not. The lead has to actually create a free account through your link, a click that never converts to a signup records nothing, and you cannot retroactively claim commission on a later purchase from that same person.
  • Being your own affiliate. Explicitly forbidden in the affiliate agreement, you cannot buy your own subscription through your own link to earn commission on yourself.
  • Losing a referral to a competing affiliate link. The attribution is lifetime, but not unbeatable: if your referral later clicks someone else Systeme.io affiliate link and buys through that instead, the commission moves to that affiliate, not you.
  • Forgetting VAT changes the commission math. Systeme.io deducts VAT (where applicable) before calculating your 60%, so a $17/mo plan sold to an EU consumer nets you less than the same plan sold business to business at 0% VAT. Do not be surprised when two identical-looking referrals pay different amounts.

FAQ

How much does the Systeme.io affiliate program pay?

60% commission on every sale, paid as lifetime recurring, meaning you keep earning 60% every month a referral stays subscribed, not just on the first payment. There is no cap on how long the commission runs.

Is there a cookie window I need to worry about?

No traditional cookie window. Systeme.io tags a referral to your affiliate ID for life once they sign up for a free account through your link, even if they upgrade to a paid plan months or years later, or switch devices. The only way you lose the referral is if they later click a different affiliate link and buy through that instead.

Do I need to be a Systeme.io customer to join the affiliate program?

No. Anyone can join for free with no application and no purchase required. You do need a free Systeme.io account to get your unique affiliate ID and access the affiliate dashboard, but the free plan is enough, you are not required to pay for anything.
