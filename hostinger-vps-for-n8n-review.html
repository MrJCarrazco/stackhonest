Short answer: Hostinger's cheapest KVM 1 VPS (1 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 50 GB NVMe) clears n8n's documented minimum and is fine for testing or a handful of light workflows, but KVM 2 (2 vCPU, 8 GB RAM) is the realistic starting point if you're running this for actual client or business automations -- it matches Hostinger's own recommended spec and gives you headroom before you hit memory pressure. Both plans include Hostinger's one-click n8n Docker template, so setup is a few clicks, not a manual server build.

What the one-click n8n template actually gives you

Hostinger doesn't just sell generic VPS boxes and leave you to install n8n by hand -- they run a dedicated self-hosted n8n template that deploys n8n via Docker in one click, with options for a plain instance, an n8n-with-queue-mode setup for higher concurrency, or a template pre-loaded with 100+ workflows. Every tier includes unlimited workflows and unlimited concurrent executions -- the caps that make n8n Cloud's paid tiers frustrating simply don't exist once you're self-hosting, because you own the server.

Pricing reality: what the tiers actually get you

Checked directly against hostinger.com/pricing/vps-hosting, September 2026, on the 24-month intro term (all plans are paid upfront for the term, not billed monthly -- the "/mo" figure is the total divided by months): KVM 1 $4.99/mo intro (renews $9.99/mo), 1 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 50 GB NVMe, 4 TB bandwidth; KVM 2 $6.99/mo intro (renews $12.99/mo), 2 vCPU, 8 GB RAM, 100 GB NVMe, 8 TB bandwidth; KVM 4 $9.99/mo intro (renews $24.99/mo), 4 vCPU, 16 GB RAM, 200 GB NVMe, 16 TB bandwidth; KVM 8 $19.99/mo intro (renews $49.99/mo), 8 vCPU, 32 GB RAM, 400 GB NVMe, 32 TB bandwidth. All tiers run AMD EPYC processors, NVMe storage, weekly backups, and firewall management. Intro pricing and exact discount percentages shift with Hostinger's ongoing promos, so the ratio matters more than the exact dollar figure -- confirm the live number before you buy.

n8n's own documented minimum is 1 vCPU, 2 GB RAM, 20 GB storage -- KVM 1 clears that on every spec. But n8n's maintainers and Hostinger's own guidance both recommend 2-4 vCPUs and 4-8 GB RAM for production use, because memory matters more than CPU once you're running concurrent workflows with larger datasets. That puts KVM 2 as the realistic floor for anything beyond testing, not KVM 1.

The 3 things nobody mentions

  • The intro price isn't the price you pay after year one. Every Hostinger VPS plan is billed upfront for the full 24-month term at the intro rate, then renews at roughly double to 2.5x that rate for the next term. KVM 2's renewal jumps from $6.99/mo to $12.99/mo -- still cheap, but budget for the renewal number, not the banner number, when comparing this to n8n Cloud's monthly pricing.
  • "1 vCPU" on the entry tier can share burst capacity under load. KVM 1 and KVM 2 aren't always fully dedicated cores during traffic spikes -- fine for scheduled workflows and webhooks with modest volume, but if you're running dozens of concurrent executions the CPU can bottleneck before RAM does. Watch your execution logs for slowdowns before assuming RAM is the only variable.
  • Self-hosting removes n8n Cloud's caps, but you now own uptime, backups, and updates. n8n Cloud's paid tiers exist partly to hand off sysadmin work. On a Hostinger VPS you get free weekly backups and a firewall out of the box, but restarting a crashed container, applying n8n version updates, and monitoring disk space are on you. If you don't want that job, the math on self-hosting looks worse than the sticker price suggests.

Who should skip it

If you're running fewer than a handful of workflows and n8n Cloud's free or Starter tier already covers your execution volume, don't bother migrating -- you'll spend more time managing a VPS than the money saved is worth. Skip self-hosting entirely if you don't want any server maintenance responsibility, don't have time to apply n8n updates, or need guaranteed uptime SLAs that a $7/mo VPS doesn't come with. If you're already paying for n8n Cloud's Pro tier and consistently hitting execution caps, that's the actual trigger point to consider this move.

Verdict

Start on Hostinger KVM 2 ($6.99/mo intro, 2 vCPU, 8 GB RAM) if you're moving off n8n Cloud for real workflow volume -- it matches n8n's own recommended spec with headroom. KVM 1 is fine only for testing or a couple of light scheduled workflows. Stay on n8n Cloud if you don't want to own server maintenance, even if it costs more per month.
