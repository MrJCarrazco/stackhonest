Two honesty flags from pulling these pages. When a vendor's page disagrees with itself, I go with the plan table -- that's what the checkout enforces.

Tool by tool

1. Pictory -- the faceless-channel workhorse

Pictory's core loop is exactly the faceless workflow: script or blog URL in, it splits your text into scenes, matches each to Getty Images/Storyblocks footage, adds burned-in captions, and reads it in an ElevenLabs voice. Starter at $25/month billed annually gets 2,400 video minutes a year, 720 ElevenLabs voice minutes, 5 GB storage, and no watermark. Professional at $35/month annually raises that to 7,200 minutes, adds voice cloning and custom avatars, and unlocks the long-video summarizer -- genuinely useful for turning webinar recordings into shorts without appearing in either.

Where it breaks: the AI's stock-footage matching is roughly 70% right, so budget an editing pass to swap the scenes where it illustrated "compound interest" with a stock clip of literal compost. And that annual-vs-monthly allowance gap means the $29 monthly plan runs dry fast if you publish daily. I compared it head-to-head with InVideo for the YouTube use case in Pictory vs InVideo -- the short version is Pictory wins repurposing, InVideo wins generation.

2. Fliki -- best voice-per-dollar, and the only real free tier

Fliki started as a text-to-speech tool and it shows, in a good way: the voice library (1,000+ voices on Standard, 500 of them the ultra-realistic kind) is the strongest of the three at this price. Standard at $21/month yearly ($28 monthly) gives 2,160 credits a year, 1080p exports, videos up to 15 minutes, one voice clone, and commercial rights. At Fliki's ~0.5 credits per minute for stock-based video, those credits stretch further than the raw number suggests. Premium at $66/month yearly adds AI-generated clips, photo avatars, and API access.

Where it breaks: the visual editor is more slideshow-like than Pictory's -- scene-by-scene with less control over pacing -- and AI avatars on Standard are a limited teaser tier. If the voice matters more to you than the pictures, also read ElevenLabs vs Play.ht, because pairing a dedicated voice tool with free editing can beat any bundled option on pure audio quality.

3. InVideo AI -- most powerful generation, least predictable bill

InVideo has gone all-in on generative models: Starter at $20/seat/month billed annually includes 400 credits, and Plus at $50 includes 2,000, spendable on Seedance video generations (~30 credits per 5-second fast clip) and image models. The full timeline editor is included on every tier, which is a real point in its favor. But do the math before committing: 400 credits is roughly 13 five-second generated clips a month. For a faceless channel built on generated (not stock) visuals, you're realistically on Plus at $50/month, and credits don't roll over. It's the right tool when you want visuals that don't exist in any stock library, and the wrong one for high-volume script-to-stock-video work.

4. The free route -- Fliki free, or CapCut and your own time

I recommend testing free before paying, and there are two honest ways. Fliki's free plan is the only real $0 script-to-video tier here -- enough to check whether the workflow suits you, not enough to run a channel (watermark, 720p, 1-minute cap). The other route is manual: CapCut's free editor plus stock footage and a free TTS voice does everything these tools do, it just costs hours instead of dollars. I broke down CapCut's free-tier terms in Descript vs CapCut. If your faceless videos need voiceover as the star, spend your first dollars on the voice, not the video tool.

FAQ

What is the best AI video generator without showing your face?

Pictory, for most people: script-to-video with stock footage, ElevenLabs voiceover, captions, and no watermark from $25/month billed annually. Fliki is the budget pick at $21/month yearly and has the only real free tier.

Is Pictory worth it for faceless YouTube channels?

Yes at regular publishing volume -- Starter's 2,400 annual minutes and 720 ElevenLabs voice minutes cover several videos a week. Know that the $29 month-to-month version only includes 200 minutes/month; the annual plan is the real deal.

Can I make faceless AI videos for free?

Only as a test drive: Fliki's free plan is 3 credits a month at 720p with a watermark and 1-minute exports. Pictory and InVideo offer trials, not free plans. CapCut plus a free TTS voice is the genuinely free path if you'll trade time for money.
