GetResponse AI Email Generator Review: Is the Built-In AI Worth Using? (2026)

Last updated October 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Everything factual here -- plan gates, use caps, prices -- was checked directly against GetResponse's live pricing and feature pages on October 2, 2026.

Short answer: GetResponse's AI email generator is a genuinely useful full-email drafter -- it turns keywords, an industry pick, and a tone into a complete email with subject line and layout in under a minute -- but the plan gating is the real story. On Starter ($19/mo) it's capped at 3 uses total, which is a demo, not a feature. Unlimited AI generation only starts at Marketer ($59/mo). If the built-in AI is your reason for choosing GetResponse, price the Marketer plan, not Starter.

What the AI email generator actually does

This isn't a sentence-finishing assistant bolted into the editor -- it drafts the whole email. The workflow, straight from GetResponse's own feature docs: you type keywords or phrases for the email's goal (a sale, a launch, a newsletter topic), pick the industry category that fits your business, choose a tone, and set a layout and color direction. The generator -- powered by OpenAI under the hood -- returns a complete email template: body copy, a generated subject line, and a relevant stock photo from GetResponse's library if your layout includes an image slot. There's a separate AI subject line generator you can run against emails you wrote yourself, and GetResponse lists multilingual generation as "coming soon" as of October 2026.

The output lands in the normal drag-and-drop editor, so you edit it like any other email. In practice that's the right mental model: it produces a structured first draft that's 80% of the way to sendable, and the remaining 20% -- your actual voice, your actual offer details, anything with a number in it -- is still on you. That matches what I've found running AI drafters for articles on this site: the draft is the easy part, the truth pass is the work.

Does it beat hand-written emails? What the data actually says

The brief version: nobody has credible public data showing AI-generated GetResponse emails out-perform hand-written ones on open or click rates, and I'm not going to invent a test result I don't have. GetResponse's own headline claim is that customers using the generator cut email creation time by 85% -- note that's a speed claim, not a performance claim. The performance evidence on their feature page is customer quotes ("it has increased my open rates on the tested emails"), which is anecdote, not benchmark.

Here's the honest reasoning instead. Subject lines are where AI plausibly helps most -- it generates variants fast, and open rate is mostly a subject-line and sender-reputation game. But the only way to know if AI subject lines beat yours for your list is to A/B test them, and that's the second catch: A/B testing isn't available on Starter either -- it starts at Marketer. So the tier where the AI is unlimited is also the first tier where you can actually measure whether it's helping. On Starter you can neither use the AI meaningfully (3 uses) nor test its output. That's not an accident of packaging; that's the upsell.

My practical read: the generator's real value is throughput, not magic open rates. If you send weekly and writing is your bottleneck, drafting in one minute instead of forty is worth real money. If you send monthly and enjoy writing, it's worth nothing.

Verdict: who should use it

Worth it on Marketer ($59/mo) if you send at least weekly, writing is your bottleneck, and you'll actually A/B test the AI's subject lines against your own -- that's the only tier combination where the AI is both unlimited and measurable. Ignore it on Starter -- 3 total uses is a demo; buy Starter for the list-building features, not the AI. Don't switch providers for it -- a built-in email AI is a convenience, not a moat; ChatGPT or Claude plus copy-paste gets you 90% of the same draft for free, minus the layout/stock-photo integration.

My own take: I'd rather see GetResponse make the Starter cap 3 per month than 3 ever -- as shipped, it mostly functions as an in-app ad for Marketer. FAQ

Which GetResponse plans include the AI email generator?

All paid plans, but not equally: Starter ($19/mo at 1,000 contacts) caps both the AI email generator and the AI subject line generator at 3 uses total. Unlimited AI generation starts at Marketer ($59/mo) and is included in Creator ($69/mo) and Enterprise. The permanent Free plan doesn't include the AI generator at all.

Does the GetResponse AI email generator improve open rates?

There's no credible public benchmark proving it does, and GetResponse's own 85% headline number is about creation time, not open rates. The honest path is to A/B test AI subject lines against your own -- which requires Marketer or above, since Starter has no A/B testing.

What does the generator actually produce?

A complete email template from your keywords, industry, tone, and layout picks -- body copy, subject line, and a stock image if the layout uses one -- powered by OpenAI, delivered into the normal drag-and-drop editor for final edits.
