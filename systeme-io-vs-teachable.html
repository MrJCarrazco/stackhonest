Systeme.io vs Teachable (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated September 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools, and I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: Systeme.io is the cheaper all-in-one option -- its permanent free plan bundles course hosting, unlimited email sends, and a funnel builder in one place, with 0% transaction fees on every tier. Teachable retired its free plan in June 2025; every current tier is paid, starting at $39/mo month-to-month (or $29/mo billed annually) on Starter, which still charges a 7.5% transaction fee on top of the subscription. Teachable is a dedicated course platform with deeper course-specific tooling (drip content, certificates, native mobile app) but no email marketing or funnel builder at any price -- you need a separate tool alongside it. If you are selling one course and want a $0 starting point with email and funnels included, Systeme.io wins on cost. If course delivery is your whole business and you want the most polished course-only experience, Teachable is the specialist.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you want course hosting, email marketing, and a funnel builder bundled for free, and you are running one integrated business rather than a course-only operation. Pick Teachable if course delivery is the core product and you want dedicated course tooling (drip scheduling, certificates, native mobile apps, an affiliate program for your course) and are willing to pay $39-89/mo plus a separate email tool to get it.

The gap is not just the subscription price -- it is what each platform assumes you already have. Systeme.io assumes you have nothing else and gives you a full funnel-plus-email-plus-course stack for free. Teachable assumes courses are the whole job and gives you a deeper course builder (drip content, certificates, native iOS/Android apps for students) but expects you to bring your own email tool and your own traffic funnel.

What Teachable has that Systeme.io does not

Teachable is a dedicated course platform, and it shows in the details: native mobile apps so students can watch lessons offline, a more polished certificate-of-completion system, a built-in affiliate program (Builder plan and up) specifically for recruiting course promoters, and course-specific analytics (completion rates, quiz performance) that a general-purpose funnel tool does not track as deeply. For a creator whose entire business is one flagship course sold to a large student base, that course-specific depth can be worth the extra subscription cost.

The catch: none of that includes getting students there in the first place. Teachable has no email marketing beyond basic transactional messages and no landing-page/funnel builder for the pre-sale journey -- most Teachable users pair it with a separate email tool (commonly Kit or ConvertKit) and either a separate landing-page tool or the Teachable limited built-in sales page. That second subscription is real money not reflected in the advertised $39-189/mo range.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

Teachable failure modes

  • No free plan since June 2025. Every tier is paid now; the only free test is a 7-day trial that requires a credit card up front.
  • The Starter tier 7.5% fee is easy to underestimate. At $533/mo in course sales, the fee costs more than just upgrading to Builder ($69-89/mo) -- below that, Starter is cheaper; above it, staying on Starter is a real leak.
  • The 0% fee on higher plans has a condition. You only get 0% Teachable fees on Builder/Growth/Custom if you route payments through teachable:pay or the Monthly Payment Gateway -- route another way and the fee comes back.
  • No email marketing or funnel builder included at any price. Budget for a second subscription (Kit, ConvertKit, or similar) if you need a real pre-sale funnel and nurture sequence.
  • A 4th plan (Advanced, $309-399/mo) exists but is not shown on the public pricing page -- it is documented only in the Teachable help center, between Growth and the Custom tier.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • Course tooling is more basic than a dedicated platform. No native mobile app for students, and certificate/completion tracking is less developed than the Teachable purpose-built system.
  • No built-in course affiliate recruitment tooling like the Teachable Builder-plan affiliate program -- Systeme.io affiliate features are aimed at promoting the whole business, not per-course.

Verdict by business type

  • Selling one course plus building an email list and a sales funnel: Systeme.io free plan -- the whole stack (funnel, email, course) at $0, versus the Teachable $39/mo-plus-7.5%-fee starting point with no email or funnel included.
  • Course delivery is the entire business with a large student base already: Teachable Builder or Growth ($69-189/mo annual) -- the course-specific depth (drip content, certificates, native apps, per-course affiliate program) earns its keep at scale.
  • Validating a first course with uncertain sales volume: Systeme.io free plan -- no transaction fee risk and no trial-expiry pressure while you find out if the course sells.
  • Need automation beyond course/funnel/email (custom workflows, API-driven agents): Neither -- see the n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than Teachable?

Yes. Systeme.io has a permanent free plan that includes course hosting alongside funnels and email. Teachable retired its free plan in 2025 -- every tier is now paid, starting at $39/mo month-to-month ($29/mo billed annually), and that cheapest Starter tier still charges a 7.5% transaction fee on every sale. Systeme.io charges 0% transaction fees on every plan, including free.

Does Teachable have a free plan in 2026?

No. Teachable retired its Free, Basic, Pro, Pro+, and Business plans in June 2025. The current lineup is Starter ($39/mo, 7.5% fee), Builder ($89/mo, 0% fee), Growth ($189/mo, 0% fee), and a Custom/consultation tier. Every plan includes a 7-day free trial that requires a credit card, plus a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Which one is better for a course creator who also needs email and funnels?

Systeme.io, if budget matters more than course-specific polish. Teachable is a dedicated course platform with no email marketing or funnel builder at any price -- you need a separate tool like Kit or ConvertKit alongside it. Systeme.io bundles course hosting, unlimited email sends, and a funnel builder into the same free plan, so a creator selling one course with an email list pays $0 on Systeme.io versus a minimum of $39/mo plus 7.5% per sale on Teachable, plus a separate email tool.
