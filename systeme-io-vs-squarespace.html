Systeme.io vs Squarespace (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated October 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools, and I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: same split as every website-builder-vs-funnel-platform comparison on this site. Systeme.io is a funnel-and-course platform with email built in -- $0-97/mo flat, 0% transaction fees on every plan, no exceptions. Squarespace is a design-first website builder with genuinely strong e-commerce and content tools, but its transaction fees only hit 0% on the Core plan and above for physical products, and digital content/memberships keep a fee all the way up to the $139/mo Advanced plan. If you want a fully custom-designed site with a store as a secondary feature, Squarespace wins. If selling a course or digital product directly is the whole point, Systeme.io free plan already does that at $0, fee-free.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you need funnels, checkout, and course hosting bundled with email at a flat price with 0% fees from day one. Pick Squarespace if the main goal is a fully custom-designed website -- blog, portfolio, restaurant, local business -- with e-commerce as a secondary feature, and you are fine paying a transaction fee until you reach a higher tier.

The honest framing: Squarespace wins decisively on design polish and general-purpose website building, and its e-commerce is more fully-featured out of the box than Wix's. Systeme.io wins decisively on wanting to sell one thing, right now, for free, with zero transaction-fee math to track. If selling a course or digital download is the actual goal rather than a side feature of a marketing site, Systeme.io free plan gets you further for $0 than Squarespace does at $25/mo plus a 7% cut of every digital sale.

What Squarespace has that Systeme.io does not

Squarespace template library and Fluid Engine editor are genuinely a different league for visual design -- the templates look professional out of the box, and the drag-and-drop canvas gives far more layout control than Systeme.io functional-but-templated builder. Squarespace e-commerce is also more complete than a typical website builder: abandoned-cart recovery, checkout on your own domain, gift cards, subscription products, and Printful print-on-demand integration are included on every plan, even Basic. For a restaurant, portfolio, local business, or content-first blog where visual quality and native e-commerce depth matter more than a funnel-and-checkout flow, Squarespace is simply the better-built tool.

The catch: that e-commerce depth comes with a transaction-fee tax that scales with your plan. A $25/mo Basic store pays 2% on every physical sale and 7% on every digital/membership sale on top of standard card-processing fees -- money Systeme.io never takes, at any plan, including free.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

Squarespace failure modes

  • No free plan. Only a 14-day trial -- there is no permanent $0 tier the way Systeme.io has, so you commit to a subscription before you have validated anything sells.
  • Transaction fees stack with plan cost. Basic ($25/mo) still takes 2% of every store sale and 7% of every digital-content sale -- the fee does not reach 0% on digital content until Advanced at $139/mo ($99/mo annual).
  • No dedicated funnel or upsell builder. E-commerce is a standard storefront; building a multi-step sales funnel with order bumps and one-click upsells means bolting on third-party extensions.
  • No built-in email sending platform. Email marketing requires the separate Email Campaigns add-on or a Mailchimp integration (Core plan and up) -- it is not bundled the way Systeme.io bundles unlimited email on every tier.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • Design flexibility is limited. Pages are templated and functional, not a creative canvas -- anyone wanting a highly polished, highly customized visual brand site will find it restrictive compared to Squarespace.
  • No general content-site tooling. Systeme.io is not built for blogs-as-the-main-product, restaurant sites, or portfolios where selling is not the primary goal.
  • No native print-on-demand or advanced merchandising. Squarespace Printful integration, gift cards, and advanced merchandising tools (waitlists, low-stock alerts) have no Systeme.io equivalent.

Verdict by business type

  • Solo creator or small business selling a course, digital product, or service through a funnel: Systeme.io free plan -- the whole sell-and-deliver stack at $0, no transaction fee to track.
  • Restaurant, portfolio, or local business where a polished custom-designed website is the priority: Squarespace Basic or Core -- the design quality and native e-commerce depth are the real differentiators here.
  • Selling physical products at real volume where even 0% platform fee still means card-processing costs matter: Squarespace Core ($39/mo, $29 annual) is the floor for 0% store transaction fee.
  • Need workflow automation beyond a website or funnel (custom API-driven agents): Neither -- see the n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than Squarespace?

For selling a course, digital product, or running a funnel, yes. Squarespace Basic is $25/mo ($19 annual) and charges a 2% transaction fee on every online-store sale -- that fee only drops to 0% on Core at $39/mo ($29 annual). Systeme.io free plan already includes a funnel builder, checkout with 0% transaction fees from day one, 1 course, and unlimited email sends, with paid tiers at $17, $47, and $97/mo flat.

Does Squarespace include sales funnels or course hosting?

Squarespace has fully integrated e-commerce and can sell courses, memberships, and video content, but charges a digital-content transaction fee that only reaches 0% on the Advanced plan ($139/mo, $99 annual) -- Basic is 7%, Core is 5%, Plus is 1%. It has no purpose-built multi-step sales funnel with order bumps and one-click upsells the way Systeme.io does at every tier, including free.

Which one is better for a solo creator selling a course or digital product?

Systeme.io, if selling directly is the main goal -- its free plan bundles funnel, checkout, and course hosting with zero transaction fees. Squarespace is the better pick if the priority is a fully custom-designed marketing website, blog, or portfolio first, with design quality and content/commerce flexibility that Systeme.io templated builder does not match.
