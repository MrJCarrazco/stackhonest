Pictory vs InVideo for YouTube (2026): Which AI Video Tool Is Worth It

Short answer: Pictory Starter is 25 dollars per month (annual) for 2,400 minutes per year and caps out at 720p; Professional is 35 dollars per month (annual) for 7,200 minutes per year and unlocks 1080p plus voice cloning. InVideo AI Starter is 20 dollars per seat per month (annual), Plus is 50 dollars per seat per month (annual, 2,000 credits per seat per month), Max is 100 dollars per seat per month (annual). Pictory meters by minutes of finished video and is built specifically for script-to-video and repurposing long content into YouTube clips. InVideo meters by credits spent across several different AI video-generation models, which gives more visual variety but makes per-video cost harder to predict. If you are running a repeatable, script-driven faceless channel, Pictory workflow is the more direct fit. If you want access to multiple AI generation engines for more varied visuals, InVideo gives you that range.

The 30-second answer

Get Pictory if you are repurposing scripts, blog posts, or long-form footage into a steady stream of YouTube uploads and want predictable per-minute pricing. Get InVideo if you want access to multiple AI video-generation models (for more visually varied, less templated output) and do not mind credit-based metering.

Learning curve / ease of use

Pictory workflow is the more linear of the two: paste a script or blog URL, or upload long footage, and it auto-generates a scene-by-scene video with stock or AI b-roll, captions, and a voiceover. It is built for repurposing, so the fastest path to a finished YouTube video is usually starting from existing text. InVideo AI chat-style prompt interface is more flexible -- describe the video you want and it generates a draft you then edit -- but that flexibility means more back-and-forth prompting to get something publish-ready compared to Pictory more templated script-to-video path.

Capability deep-dive: repurposing pipeline vs multi-model generation

Pictory core strength is turning existing long-form content (a blog post, podcast transcript, webinar recording) into a short, captioned YouTube video with minimal manual editing -- this is its whole reason for existing, and it shows in how fast that specific workflow is. InVideo strength is model variety: it plugs into several different AI video-generation engines (the kind that generate original video from a text prompt, not just stock-footage assembly), so you get more visually distinct output if you are willing to spend more credits experimenting. Where Pictory pulls ahead: predictable per-minute cost, faster repurposing workflow, voice cloning at the Professional tier. Where InVideo pulls ahead: access to multiple generative video models in one place, useful if visual variety matters more than a repeatable pipeline.

Where each one breaks in production

  • Pictory: the yearly minute allotment is a hard ceiling -- a channel publishing several long videos a week on Starter 2,400 minutes per year (about 200 min/month) can burn through it faster than expected, and 720p export on Starter is not competitive for a channel that wants to look professional; Professional 1080p is effectively the real starting tier for a serious channel.
  • InVideo: credit consumption is model-dependent and not obvious upfront -- a few experimental clips with a premium generation model can eat a big chunk of a monthly credit pool, so cost per finished video is harder to budget for than Pictory flat minutes-per-year model.

FAQ

Is Pictory or InVideo better for YouTube?

Pictory is built specifically around turning long-form text, scripts, or existing footage into short, publish-ready YouTube videos -- its script-to-video and repurposing workflow is the more mature fit for a channel publishing regularly. InVideo AI leans more general-purpose (ads, social clips, YouTube) and uses a credit-metered model across multiple AI video generation engines, which gives more creative range per video but makes per-video cost less predictable. If you are running a script-driven faceless YouTube channel and want a repeatable pipeline, Pictory is the more direct tool. If you want access to multiple AI video-generation models for more visually varied output, InVideo is the better fit.

How much do Pictory and InVideo cost?

Pictory Starter is 25 dollars per month billed annually (29 dollars per month) for 2,400 minutes per year and 1,200 AI credits per year, capped at 720p export. Professional is 35 dollars per month annually (59 dollars per month) for 7,200 minutes per year, 12,000 AI credits per year, and unlocks 1080p export plus voice cloning. InVideo AI Starter is 20 dollars per seat per month billed annually; Plus is 50 dollars per seat per month annually (2,000 credits per seat per month); Max is 100 dollars per seat per month annually. InVideo prices per credit consumed rather than per minute of finished video, so cost varies more by which AI model you use for each clip.

Can I make YouTube videos for free with either tool?

Both have limited free access to test the workflow, but neither gives you a usable, watermark-free YouTube upload for free. Pictory paid tiers start at 25 dollars per month; InVideo cheapest paid seat is 20 dollars per month. If you want a genuinely free path to faceless YouTube video, you are better off scripting in a free AI writer and editing in CapCut, then upgrading to one of these once the channel has traction.
