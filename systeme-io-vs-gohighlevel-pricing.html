Systeme.io vs GoHighLevel (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated September 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools, and I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: Systeme.io is the far cheaper option for a single business or solo creator -- it has a permanent free plan and a $17/mo entry tier. GoHighLevel starts at $97/mo with no free plan (14-day trial only), and its entire pricing structure is built for agencies managing multiple client sub-accounts, not a single business running its own funnels. GoHighLevel also layers usage-based charges for SMS, voice, email, and AI features on top of the base subscription, so the real monthly bill often runs well above the advertised sticker price. If you are a solo operator or single business, Systeme.io wins on cost by a wide margin. If you are building or running a marketing agency serving multiple clients, GoHighLevel sub-account model is the reason it exists.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you are a single business, solo creator, or coach running your own funnels and email list and want a free-forever entry point. Pick GoHighLevel if you are running or building a marketing agency and need to manage multiple client sub-accounts, white-label branding, and SaaS-mode reselling under one subscription.

The core difference is not just price -- it is what the price is organized around. Systeme.io tiers scale with contact list size and content volume, the way a single business actually grows. GoHighLevel tiers scale with how many client sub-accounts you run, because it was built as agency software from day one; even the entry $97/mo Starter plan caps you at 3 sub-accounts, a number that only matters if you are managing separate client accounts rather than running your own business.

What GoHighLevel has that Systeme.io does not

GoHighLevel packs in a genuinely huge feature set aimed at agencies: built-in Conversation AI and Voice AI for automated client communication, a prospecting tool, review and reputation management, social media scheduling, and full white-label branding so clients never see the GoHighLevel name. Agency Pro SaaS Mode lets an agency rebrand the whole platform and resell it as its own software with its own pricing, automatically creating and billing client accounts with no manual setup per client. For an agency already charging clients monthly retainers, consolidating a dozen separate tool subscriptions into one GoHighLevel bill (even at $297-497/mo) can genuinely come out cheaper than the tool stack it replaces.

The catch: almost none of that agency tooling matters for a single business. There is no need for sub-accounts with only one account. There is no need for SaaS Mode without software to resell. Paying $97/mo minimum for infrastructure built to manage other businesses, when running only one business, is the mismatch GoHighLevel own pricing page half-admits by listing Starter as "for smaller businesses and solo marketers" while every plan above it is priced and structured for agencies.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

GoHighLevel failure modes

  • No free plan at any tier. The only way to try it is a 14-day trial that starts the billing clock the moment it ends -- there is no permanent low-usage option like Systeme.io free plan.
  • Usage-based costs stack on top of the subscription fast. SMS, voice calls, email sending volume, and AI features (Conversation AI, Voice AI, Content AI) all bill separately. A $97/mo Starter plan can land closer to $147-194/mo once real usage kicks in, per third-party 2026 cost breakdowns.
  • Add-ons add up. Branded client portal ($49/mo), premium support ($500/mo), SEO ($79/mo per sub-account), dedicated email IPs ($59/mo per IP) -- none of these are included in the base plans; each is a separate line item.
  • Starter 3 sub-account ceiling forces an early upgrade. A single business only needs 1, but any agency signing a 4th client is pushed straight to the $297/mo Unlimited plan, a 3x jump with no middle tier.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • No agency or sub-account pricing model. An agency actually managing separate client funnels and CRMs will find Systeme.io sub-accounts exist but were not built with agency billing, client rebranding, or SaaS resale in mind the way GoHighLevel tiers are.
  • No built-in SMS, voice AI, or reputation management tooling. Systeme.io is a funnel/email/course platform, not a full CRM-and-communications suite -- automated SMS follow-up or review management requires a separate tool. Verdict by business type

  • Single business, solo creator, coach running your own funnels: Systeme.io free plan -- nothing to lose, and GoHighLevel has no equivalent free tier.
  • Running or starting a marketing agency managing multiple clients: GoHighLevel Starter ($97/mo) -- the sub-account model and white-label branding exist specifically for this.
  • Agency wanting to resell the platform as its own SaaS product: GoHighLevel Agency Pro ($497/mo) -- SaaS Mode and automated sub-account creation are built for exactly this.
  • Budget-conscious business scaling contacts, not client accounts: Systeme.io Unlimited ($97/mo) -- same price as GoHighLevel Starter, but unlimited contacts with 0% fees and no usage-based SMS/AI add-ons.
  • Need automation beyond funnels and CRM (custom workflows, API-driven agents): Neither -- see the n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than GoHighLevel?

Yes, dramatically. Systeme.io has a permanent free plan (2,000 contacts, 0% fees). GoHighLevel has no free plan, only a 14-day trial, and its cheapest tier (Starter) costs $97/mo, more than 5x Systeme.io entry paid plan (Startup, $17/mo). GoHighLevel also charges separately for SMS, email sending, and AI features on top of the base subscription.

Does GoHighLevel have hidden costs beyond the base plan?

Yes. GoHighLevel $97-497/mo plans cover the core platform, but SMS, voice calls, email sending volume, and AI features (Conversation AI, Voice AI, Content AI) are billed separately on usage. Common add-ons like the AI Employee plan ($50-97/mo per sub-account), branded client portal ($49/mo), premium support ($500/mo), and SEO ($79/mo per sub-account) can push a real monthly bill well past the advertised $97 or $297 sticker price.

Which one is better for a single business owner vs an agency?

GoHighLevel is built agency-first -- its entire pricing structure is organized around sub-accounts for managing multiple clients, and even the Starter plan (3 sub-accounts) assumes reselling potential. Systeme.io is built for a single business or solo creator running their own funnels, email, and courses; it has no agency/sub-account pricing tier at all on the free or Startup plans. A single business owner with one funnel and one email list gets far more for free on Systeme.io than paying $97/mo minimum on GoHighLevel.
