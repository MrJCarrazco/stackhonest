Systeme.io vs Thinkific (2026): Pricing Honestly Compared

Last updated September 2026 · ~7 min read · by Juan

Affiliate disclosure: some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up through them I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every opinion here comes from actually running these tools, and I will tell you when something is not worth your money.

Short answer: Systeme.io is the cheaper way to get a course online -- its permanent free plan bundles course hosting, funnels, and email in one place with 0% transaction fees on every tier, no time limit. Thinkific retired its free plan entirely and, on top of that, raised prices roughly 10% across its self-serve plans starting August 13, 2026: Basic is now $54/mo ($40/mo billed annually), Start is $109/mo ($82/mo annually), and Grow is $219/mo ($164/mo annually). Thinkific also introduced hard 10,000-student caps and metered video bandwidth per plan that did not exist before. Thinkific's real edge is course-platform depth -- native SCORM/xAPI support on top tiers, more granular quiz and certificate tooling, and Thinkific Payments with a tiered 0-5% transaction fee schedule that drops as you go up plans. If you want a course live today for $0, Systeme.io wins outright. If you are running a serious course business that needs deep LMS features and can absorb a $40+/mo floor with no free fallback, Thinkific's depth may be worth it.

The 30-second answer

Pick Systeme.io if you want to launch a course with funnels and email included, for free, with no trial clock. Pick Thinkific if you need dedicated LMS depth (SCORM, detailed quizzes/certificates, bandwidth headroom) and can commit to a paid plan from day one -- there is no free tier, only a 30-day trial.

The fork here is platform philosophy. Systeme.io treats a course as one product type inside a broader funnel-and-email marketing tool -- you get a course host for free, but not deep LMS features. Thinkific is a dedicated learning-management system first -- deeper course tooling, but you pay a real subscription from day one and have to bolt on email and funnels separately.

What Thinkific has that Systeme.io does not

Thinkific's course tooling runs deeper: proper community spaces bundled from Basic up, SCORM/xAPI support for corporate-training compliance needs (on the enterprise Plus tier), more granular certificate and completion-tracking options, and a declining transaction-fee schedule that rewards scale (5% down to 0.5% as you move up plans, hitting 0% on Plus). Thinkific Payments also handles tax compliance automatically in supported regions, which some course sellers value enough to pay for.

The catch: none of that is available for free anymore, and it wasn't cheap even before the August 2026 increase. A creator testing whether a course sells at all now needs $40-54/mo committed within 30 days, or the site goes dark when the trial ends. Systeme.io's free tier removes that pressure entirely -- there is no clock running while you find out if anyone will buy.

Where each one breaks (first-hand scars)

Thinkific failure modes

  • No free plan, ever, since the tiers were restructured. The 30-day trial is real but finite -- miss your launch window and the site stops serving to buyers until you pay.
  • August 2026 price hike landed on existing customers too. Monthly billers got the new price at their next cycle on or after August 13; annual billers get hit at renewal. No grandfathering unless you already had an active discount or non-profit status.
  • New 10,000-student hard cap on every self-serve plan. This limit did not exist before the 2026 pricing overhaul -- a course that scales past 10,000 enrollments now forces a move to the custom-quoted Plus tier regardless of revenue.
  • No native email marketing. You need a separate ESP (Kit, GetResponse, Mailchimp) for nurture sequences and broadcasts -- that's a second subscription on top of Thinkific's.
  • Transaction fees stack on top of Stripe/PayPal's own cut at every tier below Plus, same structural issue as most course platforms that aren't Systeme.io.

Systeme.io failure modes

  • Course tooling is basic by comparison. No SCORM/xAPI support, simpler quiz types, no dedicated completion-certificate design tools the way Thinkific offers.
  • No compliance-grade tracking for corporate/regulated training use cases where Thinkific's Plus tier is purpose-built.

Verdict by business type

  • Launching a first course, unsure if it will sell: Systeme.io free plan -- no trial deadline, no transaction-fee risk, funnel and email included at $0.
  • Established course business needing SCORM/compliance tracking or corporate training: Thinkific Grow or Plus -- the LMS depth is the point, and the budget likely already supports $164+/mo.
  • Selling a course plus building an email list and sales funnel on one budget: Systeme.io -- Thinkific requires a separate ESP subscription that Systeme.io bundles for free.
  • Need automation beyond course/funnel/email (custom workflows, API-driven agents): Neither -- see the n8n vs Zapier or n8n vs Make breakdowns for that layer of the stack.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io cheaper than Thinkific?

Yes. Systeme.io has a permanent free plan bundling course hosting, funnels, and email at $0, with 0% transaction fees on every tier. Thinkific has no free plan at all -- it retired the free tier and now offers only a 30-day free trial. Thinkific's cheapest ongoing plan, Basic, costs $54/mo billed monthly or $40/mo billed annually, after an approximately 10% price increase that took effect August 13, 2026, and it still carries a 5% transaction fee on sales processed outside Thinkific's own payments.

Does Thinkific have a free plan in 2026?

No. Thinkific does not offer a free plan. It provides a 30-day free trial with no credit card required, after which you must choose a paid plan: Basic ($54/mo, $40/mo annual), Start ($109/mo, $82/mo annual), or Grow ($219/mo, $164/mo annual). Prices increased roughly 10% across Basic, Start, and Grow on August 13, 2026.

What did Thinkific's August 2026 price increase change?

Thinkific raised Basic, Start, Grow, and its legacy Pro and Fixed Growth plans by about 10% starting August 13, 2026. Basic went from $49 to $54/mo, Start from $99 to $109/mo, and Grow from $199 to $219/mo. The top-tier Expand plan was not changed. Existing non-profit customers and anyone with an active account discount are exempt from the increase.
